# Pilot Tier Playbooks
## Execution Strategies by University Size

## Tier 1: Small Universities (<5,000 students)

### Profile
- Enrollment: 2,000-5,000 students
- CAPS staff: 3-8 counselors
- Waitlist: 100-300 students
- Budget: $10K-30K mental health initiatives
- Examples: Liberal arts colleges, small private universities

### Pilot Strategy

**Invitation Size:** 100-150 students (50% of waitlist)
**Target Signup:** 30-45 students (30% rate)
**Expected Active:** 12-20 weekly (40% of signups)

**Customization:**
- Personal touch (director knows students, mention by name if appropriate)
- Smaller cohort (more intimate, easier to manage)
- Faster feedback loop (director can see impact quickly)

**Pricing:**
- Pilot: Free 90 days
- Paid: $5/student/year × 200 waitlist students = $1K/year
- Discount: 20% early adopter ($800/year)

**Conversion Strategy:**
- Emphasize affordability ($800 vs. $70K counselor)
- Highlight personal touch (small school, we care)
- Relationship-driven (director knows founder, trust-based)

### Success Factors
- High engagement (40%+, students know each other, peer influence)
- Strong outcomes (smaller cohort, more attention)
- Director champion (personal investment, advocates internally)

## Tier 2: Medium Universities (5,000-15,000 students)

### Profile
- Enrollment: 5,000-15,000 students
- CAPS staff: 10-25 counselors
- Waitlist: 500-1,500 students
- Budget: $50K-150K mental health initiatives
- Examples: Regional state universities, mid-size private

### Pilot Strategy

**Invitation Size:** 200-300 students (20-30% of waitlist)
**Target Signup:** 60-90 students (30% rate)
**Expected Active:** 24-36 weekly (40% of signups)

**Customization:**
- Segmented approach (invite in batches, Week 1 + Week 3)
- Data-driven (emphasize metrics, outcomes, ROI)
- Scalable (show how it works for 1,000+ students)

**Pricing:**
- Pilot: Free 90 days
- Paid: $7/student/year × 1,000 waitlist students = $7K/year
- Discount: 15% early adopter ($6K/year)

**Conversion Strategy:**
- Emphasize ROI ($6K vs. $70K counselor, serves 10x more students)
- Show scalability (pilot with 200, scale to 1,000)
- Data-driven decision (outcomes, engagement, satisfaction)

### Success Factors
- Consistent engagement (40%+ sustained over 12 weeks)
- Measurable outcomes (20%+ symptom reduction, statistical significance)
- Stakeholder buy-in (director, dean, IT all supportive)

## Tier 3: Large Universities (>15,000 students)

### Profile
- Enrollment: 15,000-50,000 students
- CAPS staff: 25-50 counselors
- Waitlist: 1,500-3,000 students
- Budget: $150K-500K mental health initiatives
- Examples: Large state universities, flagship campuses

### Pilot Strategy

**Invitation Size:** 300-500 students (20% of waitlist)
**Target Signup:** 90-150 students (30% rate)
**Expected Active:** 36-60 weekly (40% of signups)

**Customization:**
- Phased rollout (pilot with 300, expand to 1,500 if successful)
- Multiple stakeholders (director, dean, IT, legal all involved)
- Integration focus (SSO, LMS, student success platform)

**Pricing:**
- Pilot: Free 90 days
- Paid: $10/student/year × 2,000 waitlist students = $20K/year
- Discount: 10% early adopter ($18K/year)
- Upsell: Universal access (10,000 students × $7 = $70K/year Year 2)

**Conversion Strategy:**
- Emphasize scale ($18K for 2,000 students vs. $700K for 10 counselors)
- Show integration (works with existing systems, not standalone)
- Demonstrate impact (2,000 students supported, measurable outcomes)
- Upsell path (waitlist Year 1 → universal Year 2, 10x expansion)

### Success Factors
- Stakeholder alignment (all decision-makers supportive)
- Integration success (SSO, LMS, seamless for students)
- Scalability proven (can handle 2,000+ students, no performance issues)
- Clear ROI (cost per student helped vs. alternatives)

## Tier 4: R1 Research Universities (Elite)

### Profile
- Enrollment: 20,000-50,000 students
- CAPS staff: 30-60 counselors
- Waitlist: 2,000-5,000 students
- Budget: $500K-2M mental health initiatives
- Examples: University of Michigan, UNC, UT Austin, UC Berkeley

### Pilot Strategy

**Invitation Size:** 500-1,000 students (20-25% of waitlist)
**Target Signup:** 150-300 students (30% rate)
**Expected Active:** 60-120 weekly (40% of signups)

**Customization:**
- Research partnership (IRB approval, co-author publication)
- Data sharing (aggregate outcomes for their research)
- Custom features (white-label branding, custom integrations)
- Advisory role (director on GentleQuest advisory board)

**Pricing:**
- Pilot: Free 90 days
- Paid: $10-15/student/year × 3,000 waitlist students = $30K-45K/year
- Discount: 10% early adopter + 5% research partner = 15% total ($25K-38K/year)
- Upsell: Universal access (30,000 students × $7 = $210K/year Year 2)

**Conversion Strategy:**
- Emphasize research partnership (co-author publication, IRB approval)
- Show prestige (other R1 universities using GentleQuest)
- Demonstrate innovation (cutting-edge AI, evidence-based)
- Long-term relationship (not transactional, strategic partner)

### Success Factors
- Research collaboration (IRB approved, data shared, publication)
- Institutional buy-in (provost, dean, director all supportive)
- Innovation showcase (university promotes as mental health innovation)
- Scalability + customization (handles 3,000+ students, white-label branding)

## Onboarding Timeline by Tier

### Small Universities (Tier 1)
- Week 1: Kickoff, setup (fast, personal)
- Week 2: Invitation, launch (100-150 students)
- Week 3+: Monitoring, reporting (weekly)
- **Total: 2-3 weeks to launch**

### Medium Universities (Tier 2)
- Week 1: Kickoff, IT review (if needed)
- Week 2: Setup, invitation prep
- Week 3: Invitation, launch (200-300 students)
- Week 4+: Monitoring, reporting
- **Total: 3-4 weeks to launch**

### Large Universities (Tier 3)
- Week 1-2: Kickoff, stakeholder alignment
- Week 3: IT/Legal review (parallel)
- Week 4: Setup, invitation prep
- Week 5: Invitation, launch (300-500 students)
- Week 6+: Monitoring, reporting
- **Total: 5-6 weeks to launch**

### R1 Research (Tier 4)
- Week 1-2: Kickoff, research partnership discussion
- Week 3-4: IRB approval process
- Week 5-6: IT/Legal review (parallel)
- Week 7: Setup, invitation prep
- Week 8: Invitation, launch (500-1,000 students)
- Week 9+: Monitoring, reporting
- **Total: 8-10 weeks to launch**

## Tier-Specific Challenges

### Small Universities
- **Challenge:** Limited budget ($1K/year may be tight)
- **Solution:** Emphasize affordability, offer payment plans, bundle with other small schools

### Medium Universities
- **Challenge:** Competing priorities (many vendors, limited attention)
- **Solution:** Differentiate (waitlist-specific, not generic), quick wins (fast setup, early results)

### Large Universities
- **Challenge:** Bureaucracy (IT, legal, procurement slow)
- **Solution:** Proactive docs (security, legal templates), parallel processes, patience

### R1 Research
- **Challenge:** High expectations (need research-grade data, publication-quality)
- **Solution:** Research partnership (IRB, co-authorship), rigorous methodology, peer-reviewed validation

## Conversion Rates by Tier

**Small (Tier 1):** 40% pilot → paid (relationship-driven, affordable)
**Medium (Tier 2):** 30% pilot → paid (data-driven, ROI-focused)
**Large (Tier 3):** 25% pilot → paid (stakeholder complexity, longer sales cycle)
**R1 Research (Tier 4):** 35% pilot → paid (research partnership, prestige)

**Overall Target: 30% pilot → paid conversion**
