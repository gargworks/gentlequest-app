## Email Performance Benchmarks

**Open Rates:**
- Cold outreach: 30-40% (personalized)
- Post-discovery: 50-60% (warm)
- Pilot reports: 70-80% (customers)
- Investor updates: 20-30% (normal for monthly)

**Response Rates:**
- Cold outreach: 20-30% (highly targeted)
- Post-discovery: 50% (interested)
- Pilot at-risk: 80% (urgent)

**Conversion Rates:**
- Email → Discovery: 20%
- Discovery → Proposal: 50%
- Proposal → Pilot: 40%
- Pilot → Paid: 30%

**Overall: 100 cold emails → 20 responses → 10 calls → 5 proposals → 2 pilots → 0.6 paid contracts**

## Automation Setup (HubSpot)

### Workflow 1: Cold Outreach
- Trigger: Contact added, status "Lead"
- Actions: Send Email 1-7 on schedule
- Exit: Response received OR unsubscribe

### Workflow 2: Post-Discovery
- Trigger: Deal stage "Discovery Complete"
- Actions: Send thank you + proposal sequence
- Exit: Proposal accepted OR declined

### Workflow 3: Pilot Reports
- Trigger: Deal stage "Pilot Active"
- Actions: Generate + send weekly report every Friday
- Exit: Week 12 complete

### Workflow 4: Renewal
- Trigger: Contract expiration - 90 days
- Actions: Send renewal sequence
- Exit: Contract renewed OR churned
