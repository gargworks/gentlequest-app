### Full Description
```
🧠 GentleQuest is a mood-first wellness app built for ADHD and anxious brains.

Most wellness apps are built for neurotypical brains. They reward streaks, push notifications, and guilt you when you miss a day. For ADHD and anxiety, that approach backfires — streaks trigger cortisol (fear of loss), not dopamine (motivation).

GentleQuest does the opposite:

✨ No streaks — we track "total active days" instead. You can never lose progress.
✨ No guilt — missing a day doesn't reset anything. You're never starting over.
✨ Mood first — check in with how you feel before anything else.
✨ Quiet by design — no push notifications begging for attention.
✨ Built for overwhelm — one-tap check-in, no 10-question forms.

KEY FEATURES:
• Mood Check-In — Log your mood in one tap. See patterns over time without judgment.
• AI Wellness Chat — Talk through what's on your mind with an empathetic AI companion. 24/7.
• Breathing Exercises — 4-7-8 breathing, box breathing, and 5-4-3-2-1 grounding.
• Journal — Quick thoughts, no pressure. Private and encrypted.
• Total Active Days — A guilt-free alternative to streaks. Every check-in counts.
• Crisis Resources — Curated helplines for 11+ countries.
• Privacy First — Your data stays on your device. No tracking. No ads. No selling data.

BUILT FOR NEURODIVERGENT BRAINS:
Every design choice considers how ADHD brains actually work:
• Task initiation is hard → one-tap check-in
• Streaks cause anxiety → total active days (never break a streak)
• Overstimulation → calm, minimal interface
• Executive dysfunction → everything reachable in 1-2 taps
• Rejection sensitive dysphoria → no judgmental language

FREE FOREVER:
Not freemium. Not "free with premium features." Free. Every feature, no paywall.

Your data is yours. Encrypted, private, never sold.

Download GentleQuest — not toward productivity, but toward feeling a little more okay.
```
