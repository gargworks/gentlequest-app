# Competitive Battle Cards - Sales Team Quick Reference
## How to Win Against Each Competitor

## vs. Wysa

### Overview
- **Type:** AI mental health app (B2C + B2B)
- **Pricing:** $3-5/student (estimated)
- **Strengths:** Established (2016), 5M+ users, clinical validation
- **Weaknesses:** Generic (not university-specific), no CAPS integration

### When You'll Face Them
- University already using Wysa (10-20% of prospects)
- University evaluating multiple AI apps
- RFP responses (Wysa will bid)

### How to Win

**Differentiation:**
"Wysa is excellent for general mental health. GentleQuest is built specifically for university counseling waitlists. Key differences:

1. **CAPS Integration:** We alert your counseling center within 5 minutes of crisis detection. Wysa shows generic hotlines.

2. **Waitlist Focus:** We're designed for students WAITING for therapy, not general population. Our messaging, features, and outcomes are waitlist-specific.

3. **Free Pilot:** 90 days free, no commitment. Wysa typically requires paid pilot.

4. **University Positioning:** We work WITH your CAPS, not around it. Wysa is standalone."

**Objection:** "We're already using Wysa"
**Response:** "That's great! Would you be open to a side-by-side pilot? GentleQuest complements Wysa - we focus on waitlist students specifically, while Wysa serves general population. Let the data decide which works better for your waitlist."

**Objection:** "Wysa is cheaper ($3-5 vs. your $5-10)"
**Response:** "True, but we're waitlist-only (500 students) vs. Wysa universal (5,000 students). Total cost: GentleQuest $5K vs. Wysa $15K-25K. We're actually more affordable for waitlist-specific support."

### Win Rate Target: 40%

**When to Concede:**
- University wants universal access (not waitlist-only)
- Budget <$5K (Wysa cheaper for small deployments)
- Already deeply integrated with Wysa (switching costs high)

## vs. TimelyCare

### Overview
- **Type:** Teletherapy platform (human therapists)
- **Pricing:** $10-15/student universal access
- **Strengths:** Human therapists, comprehensive (therapy + psychiatry + coaching), 200+ universities
- **Weaknesses:** Expensive ($100K-500K/year), requires scheduling

### When You'll Face Them
- Large universities (>15K students)
- Universities with big budgets ($500K+ mental health)
- RFPs for comprehensive mental health solutions

### How to Win

**Positioning:**
"TimelyCare is excellent for comprehensive care. GentleQuest serves a different need:

1. **Immediate Support:** We're 24/7, no appointment needed. TimelyCare requires scheduling (often 1-2 week wait for first session).

2. **Waitlist-Specific:** We support students WAITING for CAPS. TimelyCare replaces CAPS (or supplements). Different use cases.

3. **Affordable:** $5-10/student waitlist-only vs. $10-15/student universal. For 1,000 waitlist students: GentleQuest $10K vs. TimelyCare $100K-150K (for all 10,000 students).

4. **Complement, Not Compete:** Use TimelyCare for students who need therapy NOW. Use GentleQuest for students WAITING for CAPS. Both can coexist."

**Objection:** "TimelyCare provides human therapists, AI can't replace that"
**Response:** "Absolutely agree - AI can't replace therapists. That's why we're support WHILE waiting, not INSTEAD of therapy. 80%+ of our users still attend their CAPS appointment. We're a bridge, not a replacement. TimelyCare is therapy; GentleQuest is support."

**Objection:** "TimelyCare is comprehensive (therapy + psychiatry + coaching)"
**Response:** "True, and if you need comprehensive care for ALL students, TimelyCare is great. But if you specifically need waitlist support, we're 1/10th the cost and designed for that exact use case. Many universities use both - TimelyCare for therapy, GentleQuest for waitlist."

### Win Rate Target: 10%

**When to Concede:**
- University wants human therapy (not AI support)
- Budget >$100K (can afford TimelyCare)
- Comprehensive solution needed (therapy + psychiatry + coaching)

**When to Partner:**
- Propose co-existence (TimelyCare for therapy, GentleQuest for waitlist)
- Referral partnership (we refer to TimelyCare, they refer to us)

## vs. TAO (Therapy Assistance Online)

### Overview
- **Type:** Self-guided modules (videos, worksheets, assessments)
- **Pricing:** $5-10/student site license
- **Strengths:** Evidence-based, affordable, 100+ universities
- **Weaknesses:** Low engagement (5-20%), not conversational

### When You'll Face Them
- Universities with TAO already (30-40% of prospects)
- Budget-conscious universities
- RFPs for self-help solutions

### How to Win

**Differentiation:**
"TAO is a solid evidence-based platform. The challenge is engagement - typically 5-20% of students use self-guided modules even though they're free.

GentleQuest gets 50% engagement because:

1. **Conversational:** Feels like texting a friend, not completing homework.
2. **Adaptive:** AI responds to student's specific situation, not linear modules.
3. **Gamification:** Quests, XP, streaks drive return visits. TAO is passive.
4. **24/7 Immediate:** No need to find the right module. Just chat.

We're not asking you to replace TAO. Would you be open to a side-by-side pilot to compare engagement rates? If TAO works better, you've lost nothing. If GentleQuest engages more students, you've discovered a valuable complement."

**Objection:** "TAO is evidence-based, peer-reviewed"
**Response:** "Absolutely, and we respect that. GentleQuest also uses evidence-based CBT/DBT techniques, reviewed by clinical psychologists. The difference isn't evidence base - it's delivery method. Conversational AI (50% engagement) vs. self-guided modules (5-20% engagement). Both are evidence-based; one is more engaging."

**Objection:** "We already paid for TAO, can't afford both"
**Response:** "I understand budget constraints. Two options: 1) Free pilot to compare (if GentleQuest doesn't outperform TAO on engagement, you don't pay). 2) Replace TAO with GentleQuest (similar price, higher engagement). Let the data decide."

### Win Rate Target: 60%

**When to Win:**
- TAO engagement <20% (common)
- University wants higher engagement
- Students prefer conversational over modules

## vs. Build In-House

### Overview
- **Type:** University IT builds own AI chatbot
- **Timeline:** 12-24 months
- **Cost:** $100K-500K (development + maintenance)
- **Risk:** High (technical complexity, clinical validation, ongoing maintenance)

### When You'll Face Them
- Large universities with big IT departments
- Universities with AI research programs
- Tech-forward universities (MIT, Stanford, CMU)

### How to Win

**Positioning:**
"Building in-house is ambitious! A few considerations:

1. **Timeline:** Our experience: 12-24 months to build, validate, deploy. Meanwhile, your students are waiting with no support. GentleQuest can help NOW while you build.

2. **Crisis Detection:** This is HARD. 100% accuracy required (liability if missed). We've spent 6 months perfecting this. One missed crisis = lawsuit.

3. **Clinical Validation:** Need psychologists, IRB approval, peer-reviewed studies. We're doing this (SBIR-funded). Can your IT team?

4. **Ongoing Maintenance:** AI updates, bug fixes, feature requests, 24/7 support. Is IT resourced for this long-term?

5. **Cost:** Development $100K-500K + maintenance $50K-100K/year. GentleQuest: $10K-30K/year, no maintenance burden.

We're not saying don't build. But while you're building (12-24 months), would a GentleQuest pilot make sense as a bridge? Support students NOW, and if your solution is better when ready, switch to that."

**Objection:** "We want to own the solution, not depend on vendor"
**Response:** "I respect that. Two options: 1) Use GentleQuest as bridge (12-24 months) while you build. 2) White-label partnership (we build, you brand, you own relationship). Which makes more sense?"

**Objection:** "Our IT can build this in 3-6 months"
**Response:** "That's faster than most. Even so, 6 months = 600 students waiting with no support (if 100/month waitlist). GentleQuest can support them starting next month. Use us as bridge, switch to yours when ready. No long-term commitment."

### Win Rate Target: 70%

**When to Win:**
- University underestimates complexity (crisis detection, clinical validation)
- Timeline pressure (students need support NOW, not in 12 months)
- Cost comparison (GentleQuest cheaper than build + maintain)

**When to Concede:**
- University has AI research lab (can actually build it)
- Political mandate (must build in-house, no choice)
- Already 6+ months into development (sunk cost, will finish)

## vs. Do Nothing

### Overview
- **Type:** Status quo (waitlist with no support)
- **Rationale:** "We're too busy", "No budget", "Not a priority"
- **Risk:** Students suffering, symptoms worsening, crises escalating

### When You'll Face Them
- 50-60% of prospects (inertia is strongest competitor)
- Budget-constrained universities
- Overwhelmed CAPS directors

### How to Win

**Positioning:**
"I understand you're busy and budget is tight. The question is: What's the cost of doing nothing?

1. **Student Suffering:** [X] students waiting [Y] weeks with no support. Symptoms worsen, crises escalate.

2. **Missed Crises:** Students in crisis during waitlist. Some don't make it to their appointment.

3. **Reputation Risk:** If a student harms themselves while on waitlist, university is liable. 'We had no support' doesn't protect you.

4. **Opportunity Cost:** Every week you wait is another week students suffer. GentleQuest can start next week.

5. **Low Risk:** Free 90-day pilot. If it doesn't work, you've invested 2 hours. If it does work, you've helped hundreds of students.

The real question isn't 'Can we afford GentleQuest?' It's 'Can we afford NOT to try?'"

**Objection:** "We're too busy right now"
**Response:** "When is your waitlist longest? [Usually: Start of semester, post-Thanksgiving, January]. The busiest time for CAPS is exactly when students need GentleQuest most. Setup is 2 hours total. After that, we handle everything. You're busy - let us help."

**Objection:** "We have no budget this year"
**Response:** "When does your budget reset? Let's run a free pilot NOW, use the outcome data to justify budget next cycle. If it works, you have proof for budget request. If it doesn't, you've lost 2 hours."

**Objection:** "Let's revisit next year"
**Response:** "I understand. But next year, you'll likely face the same constraints (budget, time, priorities). Meanwhile, students who needed support THIS year didn't get it. What if we did a small pilot this semester (free, 100 students)? Gives you data for next year, helps students NOW."

### Win Rate Target: 20%

**When to Win:**
- Trigger event (tragedy, media coverage, student complaint)
- New budget cycle (July 1, Jan 1)
- New director (wants to make impact, prove value)
- Peer pressure (other universities adopting, don't want to fall behind)

**When to Concede:**
- Explicit "Never contact us again"
- No budget for 2+ years (confirmed)
- Waitlist <2 weeks (not urgent enough)

## Battle Card Usage

### Discovery Call
- Identify competitor ("What are you currently using?" "What else are you evaluating?")
- Pull relevant battle card
- Use differentiation talking points
- Address objections with prepared responses

### Proposal
- Include competitive comparison table
- Highlight GentleQuest advantages
- Acknowledge competitor strengths (credibility)
- Position as complement (not replacement) when appropriate

### Negotiation
- If competitor offers lower price: Emphasize value (engagement, outcomes, CAPS integration)
- If competitor has more features: Emphasize focus (waitlist-specific, not trying to be everything)
- If competitor has more customers: Emphasize innovation (we're new, but that means focused on YOUR needs)

## Win/Loss Tracking

**Track in CRM:**
- Competitor faced (Wysa, TimelyCare, TAO, Build, Do Nothing)
- Win or loss
- Reason for win/loss
- Lessons learned

**Quarterly Review:**
- Win rate by competitor
- Common objections
- Effective responses
- Battle card updates

**Continuous Improvement:**
- Update battle cards based on real sales conversations
- Add new objections as they arise
- Refine responses based on what works
- Share learnings across team (when team exists)
