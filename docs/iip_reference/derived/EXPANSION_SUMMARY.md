#### Part VIII: Week 11 (Minimum Viable Experiments - MVEs)
- **6 Key Assumptions with Experiments** ✅ COMPLETE

**Frontstage (Desirability):**
1. **Students will engage regularly**
   - Risk: Low engagement → insufficient AI data
   - Experiment: Beta test 50 students for 4 weeks
   - Metric: % who log in ≥3x/week
   - Threshold: >60% proceed; <40% pivot

2. **Caregivers/schools want real-time updates**
   - Risk: May prefer periodic reports
   - Experiment: Interviews with 10 counselors + 10 parents
   - Metric: % who say real-time is "very important"
   - Threshold: >70% proceed; <50% adjust frequency

**Backstage (Feasibility):**
3. **AI can deliver accurate recommendations**
   - Risk: AI may not be accurate/actionable
   - Experiment: Pilot with 30 students; counselor review
   - Metric: % rated "accurate and helpful"
   - Threshold: >75% proceed; <60% refine AI

4. **Platform can integrate with school systems**
   - Risk: Integration complex/costly
   - Experiment: Technical feasibility study with 3 schools
   - Metric: # where integration is feasible
   - Threshold: 2+ proceed; 0–1 pivot to standalone

**Profit Formula (Viability):**
5. **Schools will pay subscription fees ($10–$20/student/year)**
   - Risk: Budget constraints
   - Experiment: Pricing surveys + LOIs from 10 schools
   - Metric: # willing to sign LOI
   - Threshold: 5+ proceed; <3 adjust pricing/revenue model

6. **CAC is sustainable relative to LTV**
   - Risk: Acquisition cost exceeds revenue
   - Experiment: 3-month pilot marketing campaign
   - Metric: LTV:CAC ratio
   - Threshold: >3:1 proceed; <2:1 pivot on marketing

## Notable Insights from Expansion

### From Weeks 4–5 Solo Research

1. **School counselors are emotionally invested but resource-constrained**
   - Quote: "We need better tools to keep track of each student's journey. It's heartbreaking when a student falls through the cracks..."
   - Workaround: Develop own tracking methods using spreadsheets/notebooks
   - Key need: Integrated digital platform to reduce admin burden

2. **Teachers serve as first responders but lack training**
   - Quote: "I see when students are struggling, but I don't always have the tools to help them."
   - Workaround: Create informal support systems (quiet spaces, mindfulness activities)
   - Key need: Mental health training programs and parent engagement resources

3. **Parents are stressed and information-starved**
   - POEMS: Feeling overwhelmed, difficulty maintaining communication with child
   - Key need: Accessible, clear updates on child's progress; guidance on how to support

### From Week 7 Solutions

1. **Solution diversity reflects stakeholder complexity**
   - Solution 1 (Access + Partners): 10 components focused on onboarding and partnerships
   - Solution 2 (Community + Education): 7 components focused on peer support and knowledge
   - Solution 3 (Safety + Records): 16 components focused on emergency response and medical integration
   - Solution 4 (APS): 20 components focused on AI-driven personalization and gamification

2. **Common assumption threads**
   - Technological accessibility (users have devices)
   - User willingness to share personal data
   - Data privacy compliance (GDPR, HIPAA)
   - Stakeholder support for adoption

### From Week 11 MVEs

1. **Desirability risks (frontstage):**
   - Student engagement sustainability (will they keep using it?)
   - Stakeholder demand for real-time updates (do they actually want this?)

2. **Feasibility risks (backstage):**
   - AI accuracy for diverse student needs
   - Technical integration complexity with school systems

3. **Viability risks (profit formula):**
   - School budget constraints and willingness to pay
   - Customer acquisition cost sustainability
