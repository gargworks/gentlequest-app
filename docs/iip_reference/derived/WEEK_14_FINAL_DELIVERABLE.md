## 5 Key Learning Moments (Script & Slides)

### Learning Moment 1: Reframing POV to HMW
**Context:** Initial POV targeted all students.
**Critical Debate:** Broader reach vs. tailored feasibility.
**Decision:** Narrow focus to high school students for relevance and practicality.
**DD+ Application:**
- *Divergence:* Explored multiple personas and contexts.
- *Convergence:* Refined HMW question based on top 3 insights.

**Script/Notes:**
> "Our first learning moment was reframing our initial POV statement into a focused HMW question. Initially, our POV targeted all students, but critical debates led us to narrow it down to high school students. The debate weighed reach against depth. Some advocated for broader impact, while others emphasized the feasibility of targeting a specific group. We chose high school students for greater relevance and feasibility. This key decision reflected a tradeoff between addressing a broader audience and creating tailored solutions."

> "Guided by the DD+ framework, we transitioned from divergence—exploring multiple personas—to convergence—refining our target group. This process led us to craft our final HMW question: ‘How might we integrate digital tools that enable high school students facing academic pressure and mental health challenges to access affordable resources for managing stress effectively and improving their well-being?’"

### Learning Moment 2: Prioritizing Platform Features
**Process Element:** Critical Debates, Key Decisions, and Tradeoffs
**Critical Debate:** Gamification (to drive engagement) vs. AI Personalization (for tailored insights).
**Tradeoff:** Immediate engagement vs. long-term personalization benefits.
**Decision:** Prioritize gamification initially for user engagement; plan AI features for future iterations.
**DD+ Application:** Iterative testing and feedback validated desirability of gamification (e.g., reminders and interactive tasks).

### Learning Moment 3: Business Model Development
**Critical Debate:** Freemium vs. Subscription Model.
**Tradeoff:** Accessibility vs. Operational Viability.
**Pivot:** Tiered subscription model targeting institutions while offering free basic features for students.
**DD+ Application:**
- *Divergence:* Explored multiple pricing models.
- *Convergence:* Refined a sustainable strategy through feedback loops.

**Script/Notes:**
> "Another critical moment was developing our business model. We debated adopting a freemium model or a subscription-based approach targeting institutions. The freemium model increased accessibility but limited revenue, whereas subscriptions sustained operations but reduced adoption."

> "We decided on a tiered subscription model targeting institutions while offering free basic features for students. This pivot expanded our target audience to include caregivers and schools. Applying the DD+ framework, we explored various pricing models through divergence and refined a sustainable revenue strategy during convergence. Iterative feedback from stakeholders ensures alignment with user needs while maintaining financial viability."

### Learning Moment 4: Testing Assumptions with MVEs
**Activity:** Tested gamified engagement features (e.g., reminders, interactive tasks).
**Critical Debate:** Prioritize engagement metrics or mental health outcomes.
**Decision:** Validate desirability first by focusing on engagement metrics; address broader outcomes in future iterations.
**DD+ Application:** Iterative refinement of experiments aligned desirability with feasibility.

### Learning Moment 5: Iteration and Feedback Integration
**Stakeholder Feedback:** Emphasized need for community-driven features like shared stress management challenges.
**Minor Pivot:** Enhanced engagement without overcomplicating the platform.
**Iterative Process:** Cycles of divergence (gathering inputs) and convergence (prioritizing improvements) refined the prototype.
**Framework Applied:** Adaptability in innovation achieved using the DD+ framework.

**Script/Notes:**
> "Our final significant moment involved integrating stakeholder feedback through iterative refinement. Stakeholders highlighted the need for community-driven features, leading us to add shared stress management challenges. This was a minor pivot that enhanced engagement without overcomplicating the platform. Using the DD+ framework, we moved through cycles of divergence—gathering diverse inputs—and convergence—prioritizing actionable improvements. This iterative process refined our prototype, ensured alignment with user needs, and highlighted the value of adaptability in innovation."
