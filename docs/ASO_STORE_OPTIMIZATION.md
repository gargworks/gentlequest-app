
### Description (4000 char max)
```
GentleQuest is a mood-first wellness app built for ADHD and anxious brains. No streaks. No guilt. No productivity pressure. Just a quiet place to check in with yourself.

WHY GENTLEQUEST IS DIFFERENT

Most wellness apps are built for neurotypical brains. They reward streaks, push notifications, and guilt you when you miss a day. For ADHD and anxiety, that approach backfires — streaks trigger cortisol (fear of loss), not dopamine (motivation).

GentleQuest does the opposite:
• No streaks. We track "total active days" instead — you can never lose progress
• No guilt. Missing a day doesn't reset anything. You're never starting over
• Mood first. Check in with how you feel before anything else
• Quiet by design. No push notifications begging for attention
• Built for overwhelm. One-tap check-in, no 10-question forms

KEY FEATURES

• Mood Check-In — Log your mood in one tap. See patterns over time without judgment.
• AI Wellness Chat — Talk through what's on your mind with an empathetic AI companion. Available 24/7, no waiting room.
• Breathing Exercises — 4-7-8 breathing, box breathing, and 5-4-3-2-1 grounding for when overwhelm hits.
• Journal — Quick thoughts, no pressure. Your entries are private and encrypted.
• Total Active Days — A guilt-free alternative to streaks. Every day you check in counts. Missing a day never resets your count.
• Crisis Resources — Curated helplines and safety resources for 11+ countries, always accessible.
• Privacy First — Your data stays on your device. No analytics SDKs tracking your taps. No ad networks. No selling data.

BUILT FOR NEURODIVERGENT BRAINS

GentleQuest was designed specifically for people with ADHD, anxiety, and chronic overwhelm. Every design choice considers how ADHD brains actually work:

• Task initiation is hard → one-tap check-in, not a 10-step form
• Streaks cause anxiety → total active days instead (you can never break a streak)
• Overstimulation is real → calm, minimal interface, no bright colors or animations
• Executive dysfunction → no complex navigation, everything reachable in 1-2 taps
• Rejection sensitive dysphoria → no judgmental language, no "you failed" messages

FREE FOREVER

GentleQuest is free. Not freemium. Not "free with premium features." Free. Every feature, every tool, no paywall.

Your data is yours. Encrypted, private, never sold. You can export it anytime and delete it permanently.

Download GentleQuest and take the first step — not toward productivity, but toward feeling a little more okay.
```
