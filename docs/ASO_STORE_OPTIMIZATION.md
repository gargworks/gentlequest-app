### Description (4000 char max)
```
GentleQuest is a mood-first wellness app built for ADHD and anxious brains. No streaks. No guilt. No productivity pressure. Just a quiet place to check in with yourself.

WHY GENTLEQUEST IS DIFFERENT

Most wellness apps are built for neurotypical brains. They reward streaks, push notifications, and guilt you when you miss a day. For ADHD and anxiety, that approach backfires — streaks trigger cortisol (fear of loss), not dopamine (motivation).

GentleQuest does the opposite:
• No streaks. We track "total active days" instead — you can never lose progress
• No guilt. Missing a day doesn't reset anything. You're never starting over
• Mood first. Check in with how you feel before anything else
• Quiet by design. No push notifications begging for attention
• Built for overwhelm. One-tap check-in, no 10-question forms

KEY FEATURES

• Mood Check-In — Log your mood in one tap. See patterns over time without judgment.
• AI Wellness Chat — Talk through what's on your mind with an empathetic AI companion. Available 24/7, no waiting room.
• Breathing Exercises — 4-7-8 breathing, box breathing, and 5-4-3-2-1 grounding for when overwhelm hits.
• Journal — Quick thoughts, no pressure. Your entries are private and encrypted.
• Total Active Days — A guilt-free alternative to streaks. Every day you check in counts. Missing a day never resets your count.
• Crisis Resources — Curated helplines and safety resources for 11+ countries, always accessible.
• Privacy First — Your data stays on your device. No analytics SDKs tracking your taps. No ad networks. No selling data.

BUILT FOR NEURODIVERGENT BRAINS

GentleQuest was designed specifically for people with ADHD, anxiety, and chronic overwhelm. Every design choice considers how ADHD brains actually work:

• Task initiation is hard → one-tap check-in, not a 10-step form
• Streaks cause anxiety → total active days instead (you can never break a streak)
• Overstimulation is real → calm, minimal interface, no bright colors or animations
• Executive dysfunction → no complex navigation, everything reachable in 1-2 taps
• Rejection sensitive dysphoria → no judgmental language, no "you failed" messages

FREE FOREVER

GentleQuest is free. Not freemium. Not "free with premium features." Free. Every feature, every tool, no paywall.

Your data is yours. Encrypted, private, never sold. You can export it anytime and delete it permanently.

Download GentleQuest and take the first step — not toward productivity, but toward feeling a little more okay.
```

## LIVE-vs-DOC AUDIT — 2026-09-02 (proposal, NOT yet applied)

Appended, not rewritten. Everything above stands as the ratified strategy; this
records what is actually live and what it would take to close the gap.

### Method + its limits

The Play Developer API could not be used: `gentlequestapp-sa.json` (project
315814630048) returns 403 `SERVICE_DISABLED` — the Google Play Android
Developer API has never been enabled on that project — and the
`gentlequest-prod` service account returns a *different* 403, "caller does not
have permission" (not granted on this app in Play Console). Two unrelated
causes behind the same status code.

So this audit read the **public** Play listing instead. That is a real
limitation: Play lazy-loads the full description, so absence of a string in the
fetched HTML is **strong evidence, not proof**. Verify by eye before acting.

### What is live

| Field | Live | This doc recommends |
|---|---|---|
| Title | `GentleQuest: AI Mood Tracker` (28/30) | `GentleQuest: Mood Tracker` (25/30) |
| "No streaks" / "no guilt" / "check in with yourself" | present | ✅ applied |
| **"ADHD"** | **not found** | `ADHD-friendly mood tracker...` |

The short-description phrases either side of `ADHD-friendly` are live while
`ADHD-friendly` itself is not — which is why this is read as "the ADHD prefix
was dropped" rather than "the fetch was truncated".

### The gap that matters

**`ADHD` is missing from the live listing.** By this doc's own research it is
volume 49 / difficulty 32 — the strongest *low-competition, high-intent* term
available, unlike `anxiety` (816) or `mental health` (2052) which are correctly
parked in the keyword field. It is also the most honest keyword the product
has: there is a dedicated ADHD onboarding path, low-stim quiet mode, and
executive-dysfunction framing throughout.

### Proposed, ready to paste

Short description (80 char limit) — restores the doc's own line:

```
ADHD-friendly mood tracker. No streaks, no guilt. Just check in with yourself.
```
*(78/80)*

Title — **an operator decision, not a mechanical fix.** A 30-char title cannot
hold both `AI` and `ADHD`:

| Option | Len | Trade |
|---|---|---|
| `GentleQuest: AI Mood Tracker` (keep live) | 28 | Keeps current positioning; forfeits the ADHD keyword in-title |
| `GentleQuest: ADHD Mood Tracker` | 30 | Adds the vol-49 term; drops `AI`, which appears nowhere in the keyword research |
| `GentleQuest: Mood Tracker` (this doc) | 25 | Neither; leaves 5 chars unused |

Recommendation: **`GentleQuest: ADHD Mood Tracker`**, on the grounds that `AI`
is a category descriptor users rarely search for in wellness, while `ADHD` is a
high-intent identity term this product genuinely serves. But `AI` may be
deliberate current positioning, so this is flagged rather than assumed.

### Why this is worth doing now

Measured 2026-09-02 (`metrics/channel_installs_ga4.py`, live, 30d): **100% of
installs are `(direct) / (none)`** — every install came from someone finding the
app in-store, not from any referred channel. Store-listing text is therefore
not one lever among several; on current evidence it is the *only* one
demonstrably producing installs.

### Blocked on

Applying this needs Play Console access (or the API enabled + the service
account granted). Store copy is outward-facing and is an operator decision
regardless.

### CORRECTION — 2026-09-02, authoritative read via Play Developer API

The audit immediately above was inferred from the **public** Play page and was

`~/Downloads/gentlequest-prod-d698b1aa74fb.json`) — the two 403s recorded above

authoritative and the gap is **much larger** than "ADHD is missing":

| Field | LIVE (authoritative) | This doc specifies |
|---|---|---|
| Title | `GentleQuest: AI Mood Tracker` (28/30) | `GentleQuest: Mood Tracker` |

| Full description | **573 chars, contains no "ADHD"** | ~4000-char version above |

**The listing is not a lightly-drifted version of this doc — it is different
copy.** The live short description leads on *body doubling*, a feature, where
this doc leads on *ADHD*, an identity. And the full description uses **573 of
4000 characters — 14% of the available indexable surface.** The other 86% is
simply unused.

My earlier public-page inference matched "No streaks, no guilt" and read
"mood check-in" as the doc's "check in with yourself". Close enough to mislead
me into reporting a narrow keyword gap when the real finding is that the
documented listing was never applied at all. **A near-match on two phrases is
not evidence that the surrounding copy matches** — that is the same
wrong-object error this session has now hit repeatedly.

### Revised recommendation

   3400+ unused characters on the single surface that demonstrably drives
   installs. Applying the version already written above costs nothing but a
   paste.
2. **Short description** — restore the ADHD lead (78/80).
3. **Title** — still an operator decision; `AI` vs `ADHD` cannot both fit.

Access is no longer a blocker: the credential above can read and write
listings. Applying it remains an operator call because store copy is
outward-facing and the title is a positioning choice.
