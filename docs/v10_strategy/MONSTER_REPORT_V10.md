## 🔬 DETAILED FINDINGS

### 1. The "Recursive" Behavior (Emergent Intelligence)
The most striking finding was the **Autonomous Recursion**.
- **Step 1:** The `Strategist` (Mission 412) realized 100 services were too many for one context.
- **Action:** It used `brain_orchestrate_swarm` to spawn a *new* mission (Mission 432) dedicated to "Execution".
- **Result:** This hierarchical delegation mirrors human corporate structures (VP -> Director -> Team). **Nucleus naturally formed a hierarchy.**

### 2. The "Sidecar" Under Load
- **Performance:** Two dozen concurrent agents hitting the Daemon caused a **30% spike in RAM usage** (up to 1.2GB).
- **Bottleneck:** The `sqlite` WAL lock was the primary contention point.
- **Fix for V10:** We need **Sharded SQLite Databases** per active mission to prevent locking.

### 3. The "Cost" Justification (CFO Pitch)
- **Argument:** "Why pay $5,000 for one day of AI?"
- **Proof:** The swarm generated **Terraform state files for 100 services** and **CI/CD pipelines** in parallel.
- **Human Equivalent:** A human team would take 3-4 weeks to do this with high error rates. The AI did it with consistent standards in 24h.
- **Verdict:** Speed is the ultimate premium feature.

### 4. The "Failure" Modes
- **API Key Exhaustion:** The `DevOps` agent hit the `render_list_services` rate limit.
- **Dependency Hell:** 3 `Developer` agents hallucinated a non-existent Docker base image.
- **Correction:** The `Critic` caught the Docker hallucination in the verification loop. The Rate Limit require a "Backoff" strategy.
