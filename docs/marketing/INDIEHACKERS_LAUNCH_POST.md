# 🚀 Launching GentleQuest: An AI "Waiting Room" for University Counseling Centers

**Headline:** I build an AI bridge for the 6-week gap between "I need help" and "The doctor can see you now."

## The Crisis Behind the App
Hi IndieHackers! 👋 I'm Lokesh.

I'm an MBA student, but my real classroom lately has been the hallways of university health centers. I kept seeing the same tragedy:
1.  A student build up the courage to say "I'm not okay."
2.  They call CAPS (Counseling & Psychological Services).
3.  They get told: **"We're at capacity. We can see you for an intake in 4-6 weeks."**

In mental health, **time is the enemy**. Mild anxiety doesn't wait; it morphs into drop-out level depression while you're on a waitlist. Universities are desperate, but they can't hire therapists fast enough.

## The Solution: GentleQuest 🛡️
I built **GentleQuest** not to replace therapy (it can't), but to be a **resilient waiting room**. 

It’s an AI-driven bridge designed to keep students "above water" until a human is available. 

### Why it's different:
-   **Clinical Scaffolding**: It’s not a "companion." It uses PHQ-9 and GAD-7 assessments to track severity and assign targeted CBT/grounding exercises (Quests).
-   **The "Anti-Hallucination" Protocol**: As a founder, safety keeps me up at night. We use a rigid multi-agent bridge. If the AI detects even a hint of crisis, it shuts down the "chat" and forces the university's local crisis number to the top of the screen. No medical advice, just resilience coaching.
-   **Gamifying Recovery**: Because when you're depressed, even brushing your teeth is a "Quest." We award XP for small wins.

## The Build (Solo Founder Stack 🤓)
-   **Engine**: Python (Flask) + PostgreSQL.
-   **Intelligence**: Google Gemini 2.0 (The speed/reasoning ratio is unbeatable right now).
-   **Cross-Platform**: Flutter for that premium, "not-a-web-wrapper" feel.
-   **Operations**: I run an agentic backend using a Master Controller (Antigravity) that handles my auto-deploys and health checks.
