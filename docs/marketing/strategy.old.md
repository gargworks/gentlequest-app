# Unified Company Strategy (Portfolio)

> **Context:** This document tracks the strategy for our two distinct products.

# Product A: GentleQuest (Mental Wellness App)

**Mission:** To provide "gentle productivity" for anyone feeling overwhelmed, focusing on sustainable habits over pressure.

**Target Audience:**
*   **Broad:** Anyone experiencing overwhelm, burnout, or anxiety (students, creatives, professionals).
*   **Niche (Initial):** People with ADHD/Executive Dysfunction (but branded broadly).

**Core Value Proposition:**
"Tiny wins when you're overwhelmed." A non-punitive habit tracker that replaces "streaks" with "cumulative active days" to reduce anxiety.

**Key Strategic Pillars:**

*   **Content Marketing (The "Gentle" Brand):**
    *   **Voice:** Empathic, calm, objective. "The System," not "The Founder."
    *   **Keywords:** "Flow," "Growth," "Balance." Avoid "Hustle," "Grind," "Streak."
    *   **Action:** Engage in broad mental health/productivity communities (r/getdisciplined, r/productivity) with supportive, non-judgmental advice.

*   **Product Development:**
    *   **Focus:** "Anti-Streak" mechanics (Total Active Days).
    *   **differentiation:** Dopamine (Gain) > Cortisol (Fear of Loss).

**Do NOT Position As:**
*   ❌ "Just for Developers" (That is Product B).
*   ❌ "A Medical Tool" (It is a wellness tool).

*   **"Local-First" Angle:** Growing interest in data ownership and privacy. *Action:* Highlight how GentleQuest stores data locally by default, making it "therapist-proof" privacy-wise.
*   **Understimulation Burnout (ADHD Crash):** Advice for typical burnout (rest) often backfires for ADHDers. *Action:* Position GQ as the "Dopamine-Friendly" recovery tool focusing on novelty and micro-quests over silence.

# Nucleus Protocol: Strategic Positioning (Product B)

**Mission:** To provide persistent memory for AI coding agents, becoming the de-facto standard for cross-IDE context.
**Tagline:** "Works everywhere, breaks nothing."

**The "Unified Strategy V3" (from specific Debate):**
1.  **The Pivot:** We are building a **Standard Protocol** (`.brain/`), not a "Pattern Cloud".
    *   *Moat:* If other tools (Cursor, Windsurf) start reading `.brain/`, we win.
2.  **The Mechanism ("Seed & Garden"):**
    *   **Seed:** Ship `mcp-server-nucleus` with 50 "Golden Patterns" built-in to solve the cold start problem.
    *   **Garden:** Users modify patterns locally. We monitor *edit distance* (telemetry) to improve the Golden Patterns centrally.
    *   **Bridge:** `nucleus publish` CLI for explicit sharing (no default cloud sync).

**Target User:**
*   **Primary:** Solo technical founders (building with AI).
*   **Pain Point:** "Amnesia" across sessions/IDEs.
*   **Value:** Agent Identity Persistence + Context Preservation.

**Launch Strategy:**
*   **Asset:** "File-Based Brain" messaging.
*   **Channels:** Hacker News ("Show HN"), Twitter ("Build in Public"), MCP Registry.

**Recent Insights & Strategic Angles:**
*   **Self-Hosting Trend:** Developers are seeking alternatives to expensive cloud-based solutions like Vercel, creating an opportunity for Nucleus. *Action:* Highlight Nucleus as a cost-effective solution (one-time purchase) compatible with self-hosting.
*   **AI Babysitting Fatigue:** (Shared with Product A): This trend also impacts Nucleus. Position Nucleus as reducing the need for constant re-prompting and debugging of AI-generated code. *Action:* Emphasize the memory and context preservation capabilities of Nucleus, directly addressing the "amnesia" problem.
*   **"Local-First" Angle:** Growing interest in data ownership and privacy. *Action:* Pivot generated content to emphasize 'Local-First Brain'. Make this a central selling point and competitive advantage.
