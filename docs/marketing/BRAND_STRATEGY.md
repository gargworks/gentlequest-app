# GentleQuest Brand & Positioning Strategy
*Codified: Jan 10, 2026*

## 1. Core Positioning: "Gentle Productivity"
**The Shift:** We moved away from "Just an ADHD tool" to a broader "Gentle Productivity" philosophy.
**The Promise:** Sustainable growth without the guilt. Progress > Perfection.
**The Key Differentiator:** "Total Active Days" (Cumulative) vs. "Streaks" (Fragile).

### Tagline
> **"Progress, not pressure. 🌱"**

## 2. Brand Identity & Voice
**Persona:** "The App/Service" (Not the Founder).
**Tone:**
*   **Empathic:** We understand burnout and shame.
*   **Objective:** We provide a system/tool, not a personal blog.
*   **Calm:** We use words like "Gentle," "Growth," "Flow," not "Hustle," "Crush," "Grind."
*   **Visuals:** Use the App Icon (Seedling) and Confetti screenshots. No human faces.

## 3. Privacy & Persona Protocols
**Objective:** Maintain founder privacy while building public trust.

### Social Media (Twitter/X) Strategy
*   **Account Type:** **Brand Account** (Not Personal).
*   **Handle:** `@GentleQuestApp`
*   **Display Name:** `GentleQuest`
*   **Avatar:** App Icon (Seedling).
*   **Bio Template:**
    > Progress, not pressure. 🌱
    > A productivity system for sustainable growth. Build habits without guilt.
    > gentlequest.app
*   **Safety Rules:**
    1.  **DOB:** Use Founder's real year (Hidden/Only Me) to avoid age-ban.
    2.  **Email:** Use `hello@gentlequest.app` or `support@gentlequest.app`.
    3.  **Location:** "Global" or "App Store" (Never home city).
    4.  **Discoverability:** OFF (Phone/Email search disabled).

## 4. Key Vocabulary
| Term to Avoid ❌ | Term to Use ✅ |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Streak" | "Growth" / "Chain" / "Flow" |
| "Failed" / "Broken" | "Missed" / "Paused" |
| "My App" | "The System" / "GentleQuest" |
| "I built this..." | "We track..." / "The app tracks..." |

## 5. Launch Channels Strategy
*   **IndieHackers:** founder-led ("I built this for my ADHD") - *Allowed exception for "Maker" communities.*
*   **Twitter/X:** Brand-led ("We built a better system") - *Scalable identity.*
*   **App Store:** Product-led ("The Anti-Productivity App").
