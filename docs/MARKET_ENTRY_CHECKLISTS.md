# Market Entry Checklists - By Country
## Step-by-Step Launch Guides for 14 International Markets

## UK Market Entry Checklist

### Pre-Entry (Month 1-2)
- [ ] Market research (30 target universities, Russell Group priority)
- [ ] Regulatory review (GDPR compliance, UK data protection)
- [ ] Localization (crisis resources: Samaritans 116 123, NHS)
- [ ] Pricing (£8-12/student, convert from USD)
- [ ] Legal (UK-specific privacy policy, contract terms)

### Entry (Month 3-4)
- [ ] Outreach (30 personalized emails to CAPS directors)
- [ ] Discovery calls (target 6 responses, 3-5 calls)
- [ ] Pilot proposals (target 2)
- [ ] IT/Legal review (4-8 weeks, parallel)

### Launch (Month 5-6)
- [ ] Pilot kickoff (1-2 universities)
- [ ] Student invitation (100-200 per university)
- [ ] Monitor engagement (weekly reports)
- [ ] Adjust messaging (UK cultural norms: more formal, less casual)

### Scale (Month 7-12)
- [ ] Convert pilots to paid (30% conversion)
- [ ] Launch additional pilots (3-5 more)
- [ ] Establish UK presence (if 3+ customers, consider local entity)
- [ ] Target: 5-10 customers by EOY

**Timeline:** 12 months (research → paid contracts)
**Investment:** $10K-20K (localization, travel, legal)
**Expected Revenue:** $75K-300K (5-10 universities × $15K-30K)

- [ ] Cultural adaptation (indirect communication, high stigma, collectivism)
- [ ] Crisis resources (country-specific hotlines)
- [ ] Pricing (local currency, adjusted for purchasing power)
- [ ] Legal (local data protection laws)

- [ ] Localization: Traditional Chinese (not Simplified)
- [ ] Crisis: 1925 (Lifeline), 1995 (Suicide Prevention)

- [ ] Pricing: KRW 13,000-65,000/student ($10-50 USD)
- [ ] Cultural: High stigma, emphasize "stress management" not "mental illness"
- [ ] Timeline: 12 months, target 5-8 customers

### Japan Checklist
- [ ] Target: University of Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka
- [ ] Localization: Japanese language, very formal, indirect
- [ ] Crisis: TELL 03-5774-0992, Inochi no Denwa
- [ ] Pricing: JPY 1,500-9,750/student ($10-65 USD)
- [ ] Cultural: Extreme stigma, position as "wellness" not "therapy"
- [ ] Partnership: Japanese mental health NGOs (credibility)
- [ ] Timeline: 18 months (slower, cultural barriers), target 3-5 customers

## Entry Sequencing Strategy

### 2027: Tier 1 English
1. UK (Q1-Q2) - Easiest (English, similar to USA)
2. Canada (Q2-Q3) - Similar culture, bilingual challenge
3. Australia (Q3-Q4) - Timezone challenge, otherwise similar

### 2028: Tier 2 Strategic
1. Singapore (Q1-Q2) - Gateway to Asia, English + multilingual
2. Hong Kong (Q2-Q3) - Gateway to China, bilingual
3. New Zealand (Q3-Q4) - Package with Australia

### 2029: Asia-Pacific
1. Taiwan (Q1-Q2) - Mandarin, less stigma than Korea/Japan
2. South Korea (Q2-Q3) - High stigma, formal culture
3. Japan (Q3-Q4) - Highest stigma, slowest adoption

### 2030: Southeast Asia
1. Malaysia (Q1) - Multilingual, moderate stigma
2. Thailand (Q2) - Buddhist culture, collectivist
3. Indonesia (Q3) - Islamic considerations, large market
4. Vietnam (Q4) - Optional, may skip if ROI low
