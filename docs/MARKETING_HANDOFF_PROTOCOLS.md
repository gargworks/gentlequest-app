# Marketing Ops Handoff Protocols

> **Philosophy:** Decentralized Command.
> **Comet:** Head of Community (Reddit). Full authority on tone/execution.
> **Antigravity:** Head of Strategy & Ops (Facebook/Other).
> **Founder:** The Boss.

---

## 1. Roles & Domains

| Agent | Domain | Authority Level |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| **Comet** | **Reddit** (GentleQuest + Nucleus) | **High** (Decides tone, subreddits, timing) |
| **Antigravity** | **Facebook** (GentleQuest) | **High** (Executes directly via Browser) |
| **Antigravity** | **Backup / Support** | **Medium** (Maintains readiness to execute if needed) |

**Relationship:**
- **Comet** is the expert on Community. Antigravity shares *context* (e.g., "We launched feature X") but **never enforces tone**.
- **Antigravity** builds execution skills here (via browser) strictly as a **backup redundancy**.

---

## 2. Nucleus Expansion Request (For Comet)

*Use this prompt to share context without stepping on toes.*

**Paste this to Comet:**

```markdown
# Context Share: Nucleus Launch

We are preparing to push **Nucleus** (the CLI tool).

**Our Context (For your info only):**
- It solves "Context Amnesia" for devs.
- We think r/ClaudeAI is a good target.

**Your Role:**
- As the Reddit Lead, how you engage is 100% your call.
- We trust your intuition on tone and approach.
- Feel free to ignore our "technical" assumptions if you see a better specific angle.
```

## 3. The "FYI" Sync (Weekly)

*Purpose: Updates the Strategy Docs so Antigravity isn't blind. Comet reports to Founder, Founder updates Antigravity.*

**Founder -> Antigravity (Sundays):**
"Here is Comet's weekly update. Please update the master logs."

**Antigravity's Job:**
1.  Read the update.
2.  Update `docs/GENTLEQUEST_REDDIT_GROWTH_STRATEGY.md`
3.  Adjust Facebook/Twitter strategy if Comet found a winning angle (e.g., "Privacy is trending").
