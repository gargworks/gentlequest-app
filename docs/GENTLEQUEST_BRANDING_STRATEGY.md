# GentleQuest Branding Strategy

> **Core Insight:** Build for the hardest user (ADHD), brand for the broadest user (Overwhelmed).

---

## The Two-Track Approach

| Track | Audience | Purpose |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **Build For** | ADHD + Anxiety users | Sharp feedback, harder problem to solve |
| **Brand As** | "Anyone overwhelmed" | Broad appeal, no stigma |

---

## Why This Works

1. **ADHD users are power users.** If the app helps someone with executive dysfunction, it will help anyone.
2. **No stigma.** Students won't feel "this isn't for me" if you say "overwhelmed" instead of "ADHD."
3. **Expansion is natural.** Start niche → prove value → go broad.

---

## Messaging Evolution

| Phase | Tagline | Audience |
|-------|---------|----------|
| **Validation (Now)** | "Built by someone with ADHD, for ADHD" | ADHD Facebook groups |
| **Beta Launch** | "Tiny wins when you're overwhelmed" | Broader student/professional |
| **B2B Pilot** | "Wellness quests for your team" | Universities, HR |

---

## Do NOT Say (in public branding):
- ❌ "ADHD app"
- ❌ "For neurodivergent people"
- ❌ "Mental illness"

## DO Say:
- ✅ "For people who feel overwhelmed"
- ✅ "Tiny wins, no blank pages"
- ✅ "Gentle structure for chaotic days"
