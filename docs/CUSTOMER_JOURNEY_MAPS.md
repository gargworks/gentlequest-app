# Customer Journey Maps - Complete
## Every Touchpoint from Awareness to Advocacy

## CAPS Director Journey

### Stage 1: Awareness (Week 0)

**Touchpoints:**
- Cold email (personalized, waitlist pain point)
- LinkedIn post (founder thought leadership)
- Conference booth (AUCCCD, regional)
- Peer referral (another director recommends)

**Director Mindset:**
- "We have a waitlist problem"
- "Current solutions aren't working"
- "Open to new ideas, but skeptical"

**Our Goal:**
- Get on their radar
- Establish credibility
- Earn discovery call

**Success Metric:** 20% response rate (cold email)

### Stage 2: Consideration (Week 1-2)

**Touchpoints:**
- Discovery call (30 min, needs assessment)
- Demo (if requested, 15 min)
- Pilot proposal (one-pager, detailed)
- Reference calls (speak to other directors)

**Director Mindset:**
- "Is this safe for students?"
- "Will students actually use it?"
- "Can we afford it?"
- "What's the catch?"

**Our Goal:**
- Address objections (safety, engagement, cost)
- Build trust (data, references, transparency)
- Get pilot approval

**Success Metric:** 50% discovery → proposal

### Stage 3: Evaluation (Week 3-8)

**Touchpoints:**
- IT review (security docs, technical Q&A)
- Legal review (pilot agreement, FERPA compliance)
- Stakeholder alignment (dean, IT, legal all approve)
- Follow-up emails (check-ins, additional info)

**Director Mindset:**
- "IT says it's secure" ✅
- "Legal says agreement is fair" ✅
- "Dean supports pilot" ✅
- "Let's try it"

**Our Goal:**
- Facilitate reviews (proactive docs, join calls)
- Maintain momentum (weekly check-ins)
- Get pilot scheduled

**Success Metric:** 40% proposal → pilot

### Stage 4: Pilot (Week 9-20, 12 weeks)

**Touchpoints:**
- Kickoff call (expectations, logistics, crisis protocol)
- Student invitation (email template, portal announcement)
- Weekly reports (engagement, outcomes, crisis events)
- Bi-weekly check-ins (personal touch, address concerns)
- Mid-pilot review (Week 6, 30 min)
- Results presentation (Week 12, 30 min)

**Director Mindset:**
- Week 1-2: "Will students sign up?" (30% target)
- Week 3-6: "Are students using it?" (40% weekly active target)
- Week 7-12: "Is it helping?" (20% symptom reduction target)

**Our Goal:**
- Exceed targets (40% engagement, 20% outcomes, 75% satisfaction)
- Build relationship (weekly communication, personal touch)
- Demonstrate value (data, testimonials, impact)

**Success Metric:** 80%+ pilots meet targets (40% engagement, 20% outcomes)

### Stage 5: Decision (Week 21-24)

**Touchpoints:**
- Results presentation (data, testimonials, ROI)
- Pricing discussion (per-student cost, annual contract)
- Contract negotiation (terms, SLA, payment)
- Legal review (2-4 weeks)

**Director Mindset:**
- "Results are good" (or not)
- "Can we afford to continue?" (budget approval)
- "Is this worth it?" (ROI calculation)

**Our Goal:**
- Present compelling results (data > promises)
- Demonstrate ROI (cost per student helped vs. alternatives)
- Close contract (signatures, payment, onboarding)

**Success Metric:** 30% pilot → paid conversion

### Stage 6: Onboarding (Week 25-28)

**Touchpoints:**
- Contract signed (payment setup, PO, invoice)
- Production deployment (university-specific config)
- Training (CAPS staff, student ambassadors if applicable)
- Launch announcement (email, portal, social media)

**Director Mindset:**
- "Excited to continue"
- "Want smooth transition"
- "Hope it works long-term"

**Our Goal:**
- Seamless transition (pilot → paid, no interruption)
- Set Year 1 expectations (same targets, ongoing improvement)
- Build loyalty (personal relationship, not transactional)

**Success Metric:** 100% onboarding completion (no drop-off)

### Stage 7: Retention (Month 1-12)

**Touchpoints:**
- Monthly reports (engagement, outcomes, trends)
- Quarterly business reviews (30 min, strategic discussion)
- Product updates (new features, improvements)
- Support (email <2 hours, calls as needed)
- Crisis events (immediate follow-up, verify handled)

**Director Mindset:**
- "Is it still working?" (sustained engagement, outcomes)
- "Are students satisfied?" (no complaints, positive feedback)
- "Is it worth renewing?" (ROI, value, alternatives)

**Our Goal:**
- Maintain engagement (40%+ sustained)
- Demonstrate ongoing value (outcomes, satisfaction, crisis detection)
- Prevent churn (proactive intervention if Yellow/Red health score)

**Success Metric:** 90%+ retention (Year 1 → Year 2)

### Stage 8: Expansion (Year 2+)

**Touchpoints:**
- Renewal discussion (90 days before expiration)
- Upsell pitch (universal access, additional populations)
- Contract negotiation (pricing, scope, term)
- Expansion onboarding (if scope increases)

**Director Mindset:**
- "Year 1 was successful"
- "Should we expand to all students?" (universal access)
- "What else can GentleQuest do?" (faculty, grad students, athletes)

**Our Goal:**
- Renew (90%+ retention)
- Upsell (20-30% expand scope)
- Deepen relationship (multi-year contract, advisory board)

**Success Metric:** 20% upsell rate (waitlist → universal access)

### Stage 9: Advocacy (Year 2+)

**Touchpoints:**
- Testimonials (website, sales materials)
- Case studies (co-authored, conference presentations)
- Referrals (introduce us to other directors)
- Advisory board (product feedback, strategic input)

**Director Mindset:**
- "GentleQuest is valuable"
- "I want to help them succeed"
- "Other universities should know about this"

**Our Goal:**
- Leverage advocacy (testimonials, referrals, case studies)
- Deepen partnership (advisory board, research collaboration)
- Create champions (directors who sell for us)

**Success Metric:** 20-30% of customers provide referral or testimonial

## Student Journey

### Stage 1: Invitation (Week 0)

**Touchpoint:** Email from CAPS director

**Student Mindset:**
- "I'm on waitlist, waiting sucks"
- "What is this? Is it safe?"
- "Do I have to?"

**Our Goal:**
- Clear value prop (24/7 support, anonymous, free)
- Reduce friction (2-minute signup, no barriers)
- Build trust (CAPS endorsement, privacy assurance)

**Success Metric:** 30% signup rate

### Stage 2: Onboarding (First Session)

**Touchpoints:**
- Welcome screen (what is GentleQuest, how it works)
- First chat (Luna greeting, warm/supportive)
- Feature tour (mood, quests, resources - optional, not forced)

**Student Mindset:**
- "Is this actually helpful or just another app?"
- "Will Luna understand me?"
- "Is this private?"

**Our Goal:**
- Hook them (warm first session, immediate value)
- Build trust (Luna empathetic, non-judgmental)
- Show value (helpful response, not generic)

**Success Metric:** 80% complete first session, 60% return in Week 1

### Stage 3: Engagement (Week 1-12)

**Touchpoints:**
- Daily: Chat with Luna (as needed, student-driven)
- Weekly: Quests (gamification, return visits)
- Bi-weekly: PHQ-9/GAD-7 (outcome tracking)
- As-needed: Resources (crisis, self-help, university-specific)
- Reminders: Weekly check-in email (if inactive 5+ days)

**Student Mindset:**
- Week 1-2: "This is actually helpful"
- Week 3-6: "I'm getting better" (or not)
- Week 7-12: "This is part of my routine"

**Our Goal:**
- Sustain engagement (40%+ weekly active)
- Improve outcomes (20%+ symptom reduction)
- Build habit (return visits, quests, routine)

**Success Metric:** 40% Week 12 retention

### Stage 4: Outcomes (Week 12)

**Touchpoints:**
- Final PHQ-9/GAD-7 (outcome measurement)
- Satisfaction survey (NPS, would recommend)
- Exit interview (optional, $10 gift card)
- CAPS appointment (transition to human therapy)

**Student Mindset:**
- "Did this help?" (symptom reduction, satisfaction)
- "Would I recommend to friend?" (NPS)
- "What's next?" (continue using, transition to CAPS)

**Our Goal:**
- Measure outcomes (20%+ symptom reduction)
- Collect feedback (satisfaction, testimonials)
- Smooth transition (to CAPS appointment, not abandonment)

**Success Metric:** 75%+ satisfaction, 20%+ symptom reduction

## Journey Map Insights

### Critical Moments of Truth

**For Directors:**
1. **First Email:** 20% open it (subject line matters)
2. **Discovery Call:** 50% convert to proposal (demo quality matters)
3. **Week 6 Mid-Pilot:** 80% continue or rescue (engagement trend matters)
4. **Week 12 Results:** 30% convert to paid (outcomes matter)

**For Students:**
1. **Invitation Email:** 30% sign up (value prop matters)
2. **First Session:** 60% return (Luna warmth matters)
3. **Week 1:** 40% stay active (engagement mechanics matter)
4. **Week 12:** 75% satisfied (outcomes matter)

### Drop-Off Points

**Directors:**
- After cold email (80% don't respond - need follow-up sequence)
- After discovery call (50% don't proceed - need compelling proposal)
- Week 6 mid-pilot (20% at-risk - need intervention)
- Week 12 results (70% don't convert - need strong outcomes)

**Students:**
- After invitation (70% don't sign up - need better messaging)
- After first session (40% don't return - need better hook)
- Week 1-4 (60% drop off - need engagement mechanics)

### Optimization Opportunities

**Increase Signup Rate (30% → 40%):**
- Better invitation email (clearer value, less friction)
- Peer influence (student ambassadors, social proof)
- Timing (Week 2-4 of semester, not finals)

**Increase Week 1 Retention (60% → 70%):**
- Better first session (Luna warmer, more helpful)
- Gamification (quests drive return visits)
- Reminders (email if inactive 3+ days)

**Increase Pilot → Paid Conversion (30% → 40%):**
- Stronger outcomes (20% → 30% symptom reduction)
- Better ROI story (cost per student helped)
- Relationship building (personal connection, trust)
