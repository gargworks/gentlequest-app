## Full Description (4000 characters max)
GentleQuest is your always-on mental wellness companion designed to support your emotional health with empathy, evidence-based tools, and privacy-first technology.

**Why GentleQuest?**
- AI-guided conversations grounded in CBT and mindfulness
- Intelligent mood tracking with trends and insights
- Crisis resources tailored to your region and needs
- Personalized wellness exercises and daily check-ins
- Privacy by design with encrypted conversations

**Key Features**
1. **AI Wellness Chat** – Speak with an empathetic AI coach any time you need support.
2. **Mood Tracker** – Log your moods quickly, view patterns, and receive gentle nudges.
3. **Wellness Exercises** – Explore guided breathing, journaling prompts, and CBT-inspired activities.
4. **Crisis Resources** – Access curated crisis helplines and safety plans for multiple countries.
5. **Community Feed** – Discover uplifting content and curated resources to inspire your day.

**Built for Your Privacy**
Your data stays yours. Sessions are encrypted, and you control what is saved. We never sell or share your personal data.

**Made for Everyone**
Whether you're managing anxiety, building resilience, or simply want a supportive companion, GentleQuest is crafted to meet you where you are.

Download GentleQuest today and take the next step toward a calmer, more resilient you.
