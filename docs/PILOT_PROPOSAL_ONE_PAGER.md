# GentleQuest: AI Resilience Pilot Program
## Bridging the Gap Between Intake and Care

**Prepared For:** University Counseling & Psychological Services (CAPS)
**Date:** January 2026

---

### 🚨 The Problem: The "Waitlist Void"
We know the reality: **Demand > Capacity.**
Even with a full staff, students often face a **2-6 week gap** between their initial intake call and their first therapy appointment.
During this void, students feel unsupported, and mild anxiety can spiral into crisis.

### 🛡️ The Solution: GentleQuest (AI-Augmented Resilience)
GentleQuest is **NOT** a replacement for therapy. It is a **bridge**.
We provide a safe, clinically-grounded "holding environment" for students on your waitlist.

#### How It Works (The 3-Step Loop)
1.  **Daily Check-in**: Student logs mood and energy (30 seconds).
2.  **AI Micro-Coaching**: "Alex" (our AI) delivers a 5-minute CBT-inspired exercise (e.g., Box Breathing, Cognitive Reframing) tailored to their mood.
3.  **Gamified Progress**: Students earn XP/Levels for self-care, keeping engagement high (4x/week avg).

---

### 🩺 Safety First: The "No-Hallucination" Protocol
We are built by engineers and clinicians who understand liability.
*   **Zero Medical Advice**: Our AI is strictly forbidden from diagnosing or prescribing.
*   **Dual-Shield Safety**: A hybrid system protects every interaction.
    1.  **Instant Shield**: Keyword matching immediately locks the chat for explicit intent (e.g., "I want to hurt myself").
    2.  **Semantic Watchdog**: A background AI analyzes every message for *implicit* distress (e.g., "I don't know if I can keep going") and alerts staff asynchronously without blocking the user.
*   **Privacy**: GDPR/CCPA compliant. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
