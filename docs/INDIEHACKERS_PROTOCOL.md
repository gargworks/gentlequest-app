# IndieHackers Engagement Protocol

> **Goal:** Get "Roast" feedback on GentleQuest concept/onboarding from fellow builders.
> **Platform:** IndieHackers.com & r/indiehackers

---

## 1. The Strategy: "Vulnerable Builder"

IndieHackers hate "marketing." They love "building in public" and "seeking truth."
**Tone:** Humble, curious, technical, transparent about flaws.

---

## 2. Posting Rules

**Title Formats:**
- ✅ "Roast my onboarding flow for ADHD users"
- ✅ "Is this gamification too cringe? (Mental Health App)"
- ✅ "Solo founder seeking feedback: Quests vs Chatbots"

**Content Guardrails:**
- **No Marketing Speak:** Don't say "Revolutionary," "Best," "Unique."
- **Show, Don't Tell:** Include a link to screenshots or specialized landing page.
- **Ask Specific Questions:** "Does step 3 feel too slow?" vs "Do you like it?"

---

## 3. Engagement Protocol

**When they Roast (Criticize):**
- **Acceptance:** "That's a great point. I missed that."
- **Curiosity:** "How would you handle the 'empty state' problem instead?"
- **No Defense:** Do NOT argue. Do NOT sell features.

**The "Beta Tester" Conversion:**
- If someone gives great feedback: "This is super helpful. Would you be open to a 15-min deep dive? I can give you free Pro access for life."
