## Break-Even Analysis

### Monthly Break-Even

**Fixed Costs:**
- Tools: $500/month
- Marketing: $500/month
- Legal: $500/month
- **Total: $1,500/month**

**Variable Costs:**
- AI API: $0.50/student/month
- Hosting: $0.10/student/month
- **Total: $0.60/student/month**

**Revenue per Student:**
- $10/student/year = $0.83/student/month

**Contribution Margin:**
- $0.83 - $0.60 = $0.23/student/month

**Break-Even Students:**
- $1,500 fixed ÷ $0.23 margin = 6,522 students
- OR: 7 universities × 1,000 students each

**With Team (2027):**
- Fixed: $20K/month (salaries + tools)
- Break-even: 87,000 students OR 87 universities

**Conclusion:** Need scale (40+ universities) OR higher pricing ($15-20/student) for profitability with team

### Annual Break-Even

**2026 (Solo Founder):**
- Fixed: $18K/year (tools, marketing, legal)
- Variable: $7.20/student/year ($0.60 × 12)
- Revenue: $10/student/year
- Margin: $2.80/student/year
- **Break-even: 6,429 students OR 6-7 universities**

**2027 (With Team):**
- Fixed: $240K/year (salaries, tools, marketing)
- Margin: $2.80/student/year
- **Break-even: 85,714 students OR 86 universities**

**Realistic (2027):** 25 universities, 25K students, $250K revenue, marginally profitable
