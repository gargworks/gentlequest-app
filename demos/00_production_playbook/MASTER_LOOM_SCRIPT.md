## Production Notes
*   **Voiceover:** Natural, confident, slightly fast-paced. Like you're showing a friend a cool trick.
*   **Audio:** Subtle keyboard clacks (ASMR style) under the voiceover. "Glitch" sound effect on the "Governance Lockout" and the "Snap".
*   **Visuals:** Keep the "Split Zone" active for Shorts (Top: UI, Bottom: Terminal).
*   **Pacing:** Don't let it drag. If a loading spinner takes too long, speed it up (Time Warp).
