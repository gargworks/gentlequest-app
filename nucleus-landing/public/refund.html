Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-04

This Cancellation & Refund Policy applies to all Eidetic Works paid subscriptions, including Eidetic Pro, Sovereign Studio, and any future paid tiers purchased through our checkout. It supplements (but does not override) the broader Terms of Service.

1. Cancellation

You may cancel any Eidetic Works subscription at any time. To cancel:

  1. Open the subscription-management portal link sent in your receipt email, or in any subsequent renewal-notice email.
  2. Click Cancel subscription.
  3. Your cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You retain access until that date and you will not be charged again.

If you cannot find the portal link, email hi@eidetic.works and we will help you within 1 business day.

2. 7-day money-back guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your subscription, you may request a full refund within 7 days of your first paid purchase. No questions asked. Email hi@eidetic.works with your receipt and we will process the refund within 7 business days.

This 7-day guarantee applies separately for each distinct Eidetic Works subscription (Eidetic Pro and Sovereign Studio are separate products with separate 7-day windows).

3. Renewal charges

For renewal charges after the first 7-day window, we do not generally issue refunds for partial periods. You may cancel future renewals at any time and continue using the service until the end of the paid period.

If a renewal was charged due to a technical error — for example, you cancelled but the charge went through anyway — email hi@eidetic.works with the receipt and we will refund the renewal within 7 business days.

4. Exceptional circumstances

We will consider refunds outside the 7-day window in exceptional circumstances, such as:

  • A documented service outage affecting you specifically
  • An unauthorised charge on your account
  • A material defect in the service that significantly affects your usage and that we cannot remedy in a reasonable time
  • Other circumstances we consider in good faith

Email hi@eidetic.works with details. We respond within 1 business day.

5. Indian consumer rights

If you are an Indian consumer under the Consumer Protection Act 2019, the above policy does not limit your statutory rights. You may approach a Consumer Forum under the Act if a dispute cannot be resolved with us directly. We encourage you to email us first as most disputes resolve without formal proceedings.

6. EU consumer right of withdrawal

If you are an EU/UK consumer, under the EU Consumer Rights Directive (Directive 2011/83/EU) or equivalent UK legislation, you have a 14-day right of withdrawal for distance contracts. Our 7-day money-back guarantee runs in parallel with this 14-day statutory right, meaning EU/UK consumers effectively have the longer 14-day window. To exercise this right, email hi@eidetic.works within 14 days of your first paid purchase.

Please note: for digital content downloaded immediately on purchase, the right of withdrawal can be waived if you explicitly request immediate delivery and acknowledge the loss of the withdrawal right. For Sovereign Studio and Eidetic Pro, your subscription gives access to ongoing service rather than a one-time download, so the standard 14-day withdrawal right applies.

7. How refunds are processed

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. The refund typically appears on your statement within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer. We do not issue refunds in any other form than to the original payment method.

8. Apple App Store purchases

If you ever purchase a subscription through Apple's in-app purchase flow (rather than our website checkout), refunds for those purchases are governed by Apple's own policies, not ours. Request such refunds through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

9. Currency conversion

Eidetic Works subscriptions are priced in US Dollars (USD). If your card or bank account is denominated in a different currency, your card issuer will perform the conversion at their prevailing rate. Refunds are processed in the original currency of charge; any difference between the original charge and the refund due to exchange-rate movement is borne by the customer's bank.

10. Tax

Subscription prices are inclusive of applicable taxes unless otherwise indicated at checkout. Where we are required to collect tax (for example, EU VAT or India GST as applicable), refunds will include the associated tax amount.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cancellation & Refund Policy from time to time. Material changes will be emailed to active subscribers at least 14 days before taking effect. Continuing to use the service after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
