Support

We're a small team and we read every message. Email is the fastest way to reach us, and we respond within 1 business day Monday through Friday.

2. Product-specific help

Eidetic CLI / daemon (free)

The Eidetic CLI runs locally and captures records from your Claude Code, Cursor, and other AI sessions. If eideticd won't start, check the install guide in the public repo at github.com/eidetic-works/eidetic-daemon. For runtime issues, run eideticd diagnose and email us the output.

Eidetic Pro (paid subscription)

Eidetic Pro adds cross-device sync, R2 cloud backup, restore-in-60s, and the nucleus_ask recall path on top of the free Eidetic CLI. Manage your subscription from the dashboard linked in your receipt email, or email hi@eidetic.works.

Sovereign Voice (free Mac app)

Sovereign Voice runs a private speech-synthesis engine on your Apple Silicon Mac. No account, no internet required after install, no telemetry. If the app doesn't open after install, see the GitHub release notes or email hi@eidetic.works.

Sovereign Studio (paid subscription, $9/mo)

Sovereign Studio is the paid upgrade for Sovereign Voice on Mac. You'll see the charge as EIDETIC WORKS* SOVEREIGN on your bank or card statement. Manage or cancel anytime from the subscription portal sent in your receipt email — see our cancellation and refund policy for details.

Sovereign Voice iPhone companion

Pairs with the Sovereign Voice Mac daemon over Wi-Fi or via the cloud tunnel. If you can't find your Mac, ensure Sovereign Voice is running on the Mac and your devices are on the same network. Alternatively, use "Enter IP manually" in the companion app.

3. Account & billing

All Eidetic Works subscriptions (Eidetic Pro, Sovereign Studio, and any other paid tiers) are managed through our payment processor's customer portal. The link is included in every receipt and renewal-notice email — there you can update payment methods, cancel subscriptions, or download past invoices.

If you can't find a receipt, email hi@eidetic.works with the date of purchase and we'll help you within 1 business day.

Email privacy@eidetic.works with your request and we will respond within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

By using this service, you agree that this email-mediated address-request mechanism satisfies any notice-address requirement applicable to pre-launch operations of this size; once we reach commercial scale (first 100 paid customers or first enterprise contract), we will publish a permanent address.

We aim to respond to every email within 1 business day (Mon–Fri). Complex issues may take longer to resolve, but we'll acknowledge your message within 1 business day and let you know an expected timeline.
