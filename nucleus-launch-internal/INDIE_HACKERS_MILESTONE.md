### 📝 Milestone Title
**Public Launch: Nucleus MCP is live on Product Hunt!**

### 📝 Milestone Description
Today is the day! We’ve officially taken Nucleus MCP from a internal "Poison Pill" prototype to a public local-first Agentic OS.

Over the last few weeks, we've focused on one goal: **Sovereignty.**
AI agents are getting smarter, but they don't have a persistent brain or a security layer. Nucleus gives them both, keeping everything 100% local on your machine.

**Why we’re excited:**
*   **The Hypervisor**: Real governance for autonomous agents.
*   **The Shared Brain**: Syncing context across Cursor, Claude, and Windsurf instantly.
*   **Recursive Mounting**: One command to orchestrate your entire tool stack.

We’re live on Product Hunt today and would love the support of the IH community as we build the secure foundation for the Agentic Age.
