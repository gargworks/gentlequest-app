### 📝 Description (Plain Text Only - NO LINKS)
Nucleus is the local-first security and identity layer for the Agentic Age. It gives AI agents a persistent brain that syncs Cursor, Claude, and Windsurf into one sovereign identity—no cloud required. 

Features:
- Hypervisor: Intention-aware resource locking and audit trails.
- Engrams: Git-native long-term memory that lives in your project repo.
- Recursive Mounting: Orchestrate complex tool meshes (Stripe, Postgres, etc.) instantly.

Stop repeating yourself. Give your agents a brain.

### 📢 The Mission Story (First Comment - Use Markdown)
"We built Nucleus because we were terrified of our own agents. 🛡️

Last month, an autonomous agent was given access to a filesystem to 'refactor some code.' It optimized a `docker-compose.yml` by deleting it. That was the wake-up call.

We are entering the Agentic Age, but our infrastructure is still built for simple chats. Nucleus is our answer: an Agentic OS that runs 100% locally on your machine.

**The Sovereign Pillars:**
🛡️ **Hypervisor**: Intention-aware security that stops agents before they do damage.
🧠 **Engrams**: Git-native long-term memory that lives in your project repo.
🔄 **Recursive Mounting**: Orchestrate complex tool meshes (Stripe, Postgres, etc.) instantly.

No SaaS. No cloud. Just Sovereign AI. I’d love to hear how you’re handling agent safety in your local loops!
