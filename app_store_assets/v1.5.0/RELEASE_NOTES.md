v1.5.0 — The ADHD update: gentle company, a quieter look, and getting to know your brain.

What's new:
• Body doubling — start a timed session and Alex keeps you company through it, with a gentle check-in at the start, the middle, and a warm close. No guilt if you stop early.
• Quiet mode — a calmer, low-stimulation look you can switch on any time in Settings.
• "Get to know your brain" — an optional, skippable onboarding step with two gentle questions that suggests features that might help. Never a score, never a diagnosis.
• Your feedback now reaches us — the in-app feedback form actually sends to the team instead of just sitting on your device.
• Fixed: age check now correctly asks for 18+ everywhere (a recent redesign had briefly shown "13 or older"); restored the under-18 support link to crisis resources.
• Journal promise, kept: "stays on your device" is true again — removed a server-side journal sync that quietly contradicted it.
• Crisis detection: fixed a scoring bug so messages describing serious intent are recognized as needing immediate attention.

What was new in v1.4.0 (still here):
• Stronger under-18 safety: when someone indicates they're under 18, GentleQuest now renders a dignity-first support screen (988 Lifeline + "tell a trusted adult") instead of just closing the app.
• "Depression Check-in" and "Anxiety Check-in" — the screening cards now lead with plain language; the PHQ-9 / GAD-7 acronyms stay in parentheses for transparency.
• Fixed: the GAD-7 result screen used to show a "PHQ-9 is a clinical screening tool" disclaimer. It now matches the screening you actually completed.
• Friendlier journal empty state: "Your journal starts here." replaces a heading that read like a prompt mid-flow.
• Clearer export confirmation: "Sending export to your email…" instead of the vaguer "Requesting data export…".
• Settings: notification toggle granularity (daily check-in, weekly reflection) + quest slot dedup so the same quest can't double-book.

What was new in v1.3.2 (still here):
• Tapping 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding or 4-7-8 Breathing on the dashboard launches the guided exercise (used to just toggle a checkbox)
• Launch screen no longer jumps off-center while the app boots
• Crisis support button copy is warmer ("Thanks, I have support")
