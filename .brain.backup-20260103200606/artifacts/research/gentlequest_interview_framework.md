## Interview Script: D2C Users (Students/Individuals)

### Setup (2 min)

> "Hi! I'm researching how college students manage stress and mental health. I'm not selling anything — just trying to understand real experiences. This is anonymous and takes about 15 minutes. Is that okay?"

### Section 1: Current State (5 min)

**Q1: Daily Experience**
> "How would you describe your mental health over the past month? What's been on your mind?"

*Listen for:* Stress, anxiety, loneliness, academic pressure, sleep issues

**Q2: The Moment of Need**
> "Tell me about the last time you really needed to talk to someone about how you were feeling. What happened?"

*Listen for:* Who they reached out to, barriers, what helped/didn't

**Q3: Current Resources**
> "What do you currently do when you're feeling overwhelmed or anxious?"

*Listen for:* Apps, friends, journaling, nothing, unhealthy coping

### Section 2: Pain Points (5 min)

**Q4: Access to Care**
> "Have you ever tried to get professional mental health support? What was that experience like?"

*Listen for:* Waitlists, cost, stigma, didn't know how

**Q5: The Gap**
> "What's the biggest gap between how you feel and the support you can actually get?"

*Listen for:* "No one available at 2am", "Can't afford therapy", "Don't want to burden friends"

**Q6: App Experience** (if applicable)
> "Have you tried any mental health apps like Calm, Headspace, or Wysa? What did you like or dislike?"

*Listen for:* "Too generic", "Didn't stick with it", "Meditation isn't for me"

### Section 3: Solution Validation (5 min)

**Q7: The Magic Wand**
> "If you could have an AI companion that actually remembered your story, tracked your mood, and suggested personalized activities based on how you're feeling — would that be helpful?"

*Listen for:* Excitement? Skepticism? What features matter?

**Q8: Gamification Response**
> "What if it turned self-care into a game — like completing 'quests' for breathing exercises or journaling — and you could see your progress over time?"

*Listen for:* "That sounds fun" vs "Seems gimmicky"

**Q9: Trust & Privacy**
> "What would make you trust an AI with your mental health information?"

*Listen for:* Privacy, no data selling, local storage, no judgment

**Q10: Willingness to Pay**
> "If this app worked well for you, would you pay $5-10/month for it? Or would you expect it free?"

*Listen for:* Price sensitivity, what would justify payment

### Closer (2 min)

> "Thanks so much! We're building something called GentleQuest — an AI companion named Luna that does exactly what we discussed. Can I email you when we launch the beta?"

*Collect:* Email (for waitlist), permission to follow up

## Interview Script: B2B Buyers (University Staff)

### Setup (2 min)

> "Hi [Name], thanks for taking the time. I'm researching how universities are approaching the student mental health challenge. I'd love to understand your experience — this isn't a sales call."

### Section 1: Current Landscape (5 min)

**Q1: Demand Reality**
> "How has demand for counseling services changed over the past few years at [University]?"

*Listen for:* Volume numbers, waitlist length, crisis frequency

**Q2: Capacity Challenge**
> "What's your current waitlist situation? How long are students waiting for an initial appointment?"

*Listen for:* Days/weeks, triage process, what happens in the gap

**Q3: Current Technology**
> "Are you using any digital mental health tools currently? (Headspace, Calm, Wysa, etc.)"

*Listen for:* What's working, what's not, adoption rates

### Section 2: Pain Points (5 min)

**Q4: The Biggest Gap**
> "What's the biggest unmet need you see for students right now in terms of mental health support?"

*Listen for:* After-hours support, mild-moderate cases, prevention

**Q5: Tech Skepticism**
> "What concerns do you have about digital mental health tools?"

*Listen for:* Evidence, privacy, liability, replaces human care

**Q6: Procurement Reality**
> "If you wanted to bring in a new tool, what does that process look like?"

*Listen for:* Decision makers, timeline, budget cycles, IT requirements

### Section 3: Solution Validation (5 min)

**Q7: The Pitch**
> "Imagine an AI companion that provides 24/7 support, uses evidence-based techniques like CBT and DBT, tracks PHQ-9/GAD-7 outcomes automatically, and escalates crisis situations to your team. Would that be valuable?"

*Listen for:* Interest level, which features matter most

**Q8: Outcome Tracking**
> "How important is it that a tool provides measurable outcomes you can use for accreditation or reporting?"

*Listen for:* "Essential" or "Nice to have"

**Q9: Pilot Interest**
> "Would you consider a 90-day free pilot to test something like this with a cohort of students?"

*Listen for:* Yes/No, barriers, who else needs to approve

**Q10: Price Sensitivity**
> "What would be a reasonable per-student cost for a tool that reduces waitlist pressure and provides 24/7 coverage?"

*Listen for:* Per-student-per-year expectations, budget reality

### Closer (2 min)

> "Thanks so much for your insights. We're building GentleQuest — would you be open to seeing a demo when we're ready for university pilots?"

*Collect:* Email, best follow-up timing, other contacts
