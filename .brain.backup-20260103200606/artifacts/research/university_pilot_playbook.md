## Executive Summary

University mental health pilots succeed through **three key relationships**: Counseling Center Director (clinical validation), Dean of Students (budget authority), and Student Government (grassroots demand). Start with 1-3 small/medium schools, offer 90-day free pilots, track PHQ-9/GAD-7 outcomes for case study.

### Primary Target: Counseling Center Director

- Member of AUCCCD (Association for University and College Counseling Center Directors)
- Overwhelmed by demand (5x growth vs enrollment)
- Open to tech but skeptical of unproven tools
- **Key objection:** "Where's the evidence?"

## Outreach Strategy

### Phase 1: Target Selection

**Ideal First Universities:**
- Enrollment: 5,000-20,000 students (not too big, not too small)
- Location: Founder's network (warm intros)
- Situation: Known counseling capacity issues
- Type: Public or private (both viable)

### Phase 2: Cold Outreach Template

**Subject:** Reducing Waitlist Pressure with AI Companion — 90-Day Pilot

**Email:**

> Hi [Director's Name],
>
> I noticed [University] has been expanding mental health support — great to see the investment in student wellbeing.
>
> We've built GentleQuest, an AI companion that provides 24/7 wellness support using evidence-based techniques (CBT, DBT, ACT). We're offering free 90-day pilots to 5 universities this semester.
>
> What we offer:
> - 24/7 AI support for stress, anxiety, mild depression
> - Crisis detection with escalation to your counseling center
> - PHQ-9/GAD-7 tracking for outcome measurement
> - Privacy-first (HIPAA-aligned, no data selling)
>
> We're NOT trying to replace counselors — we're trying to help students stuck on waitlists or too anxious to walk into the counseling center.
>
> Would you be open to a 15-minute call to explore fit?
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]
> Founder, GentleQuest
> [Phone] | [LinkedIn]

## Handling Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "No evidence it works" | "That's exactly why we want to run a pilot — to generate evidence together. We'll track PHQ-9/GAD-7 outcomes." |
| "Students won't use another app" | "We've found 20-40% engagement in early tests. Quest gamification drives return visits." |
| "Privacy concerns" | "We're HIPAA-aligned, no data selling, encryption at rest/transit. Happy to go through IT security review." |
| "We already have Headspace/Calm" | "Great for meditation, but not intervention-focused. We complement those — Luna actually DOES things like mood logging and crisis detection." |
| "Budget is frozen" | "This pilot is free. We're investing in building case studies. If ROI is proven, we can discuss pricing for next year." |
