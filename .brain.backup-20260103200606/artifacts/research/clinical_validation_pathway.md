## HIPAA and Privacy Compliance

### Does HIPAA Apply to GentleQuest?

| Scenario | HIPAA Applies? |
|----------|----------------|
| D2C app with no healthcare ties | ❌ No (FTC governs) |
| B2B with healthcare provider | ✅ Yes |
| University counseling center partnership | ✅ Likely yes |

### Requirements When HIPAA Applies

| Area | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Encryption** | AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit |
| **Access Control** | MFA, role-based access |
| **BAA** | Business Associate Agreement required |
| **Audit Trails** | Log all PHI access |
| **Risk Assessment** | Regular security audits |

### FTC Enforcement (When HIPAA Doesn't Apply)

| Action | Example |
