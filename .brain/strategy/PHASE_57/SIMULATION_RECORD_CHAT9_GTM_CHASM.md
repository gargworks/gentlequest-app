## 1. The Framework (Geoffrey Moore)

We identified the critical gap between our current users (Early Adopters) and the sustainable market (Early Majority).

### The Gap Analysis
*   **Early Adopters (Visionaries):**
    *   *Who:* AI Hackers, Research Scientists.
    *   *Why they buy:* "It's Sovereign AI! It's cool technology."
    *   *Tolerance:* High. They will fix our yaml bugs.
*   **The Chasm:** The gap where products die.
*   **Early Majority (Pragmatists):**
    *   *Who:* DevOps Engineers, Senior Platform Architects.
    *   *Why they buy:* "It solves my Secrets Management problem securely."
    *   *Tolerance:* Zero. It must work 100% of the time.

---

## 2. The Beachhead Strategy (Pick One Niche)

We cannot attack the whole market. We must dominate one vertical to cross the Chasm.

*   **Option A: Technical Writers.** (Docs -> Code sync). *Too small.*
*   **Option B: Data Scientists.** (Local GPU usage). *Crowded (Ollama dominates).*
*   **Option C: DevOps/Platform.** (Secure Automation). *Perfect Fit.*

**Selection: The DevOps Beachhead.**
*   *Pain Point:* "I have 50 scripts to manage AWS/GCP. Sharing them is unsafe. Running them locally is messy."
*   *Solution:* Nucleus as the "Secure Ops Runtime".
