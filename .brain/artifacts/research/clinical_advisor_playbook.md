# Clinical Advisor Recruitment Playbook

**Goal:** Recruit 2 Clinical Advisors to validate GentleQuest's safety protocols and lend credibility for B2B pilots.
**Target Profile:** "The Pragmatic Clinician" (Open to AI, understands liability, but wants to solve the access crisis).

---

## 1. Ideal Advisor Profiles

### A. The "University Insider" (B2B Credibility)
*   **Role:** Director of Counseling Services or similar.
*   **Why:** They sign the checks for our B2B product. They know the liability fears inside out.
*   **Where to find:** AUCCCD (Association for University and College Counseling Center Directors), LinkedIn (search "Director Counseling Services").
*   **Ask:** "Review our Crisis Escalation Protocol to ensure it meets your liability standards."

### B. The "Digital Health Scholar" (Clinical Validity)
*   **Role:** PhD/PsyD Researcher in Digital Interventions.
*   **Why:** They know how to structure the PHQ-9 data to make it publishable (RCTs).
*   **Where to find:** Digital Health Labs (UCL, King's College, U of Toronto), Google Scholar (Search "smartphone intervention anxiety").
*   **Ask:** "Help us design our 90-day pilot data collection so we can publish a paper together."

---

## 2. Compensation Benchmarks (Mental Health Startup)

| Engagement Level | Equity Range (Vesting 2 yrs) | Interaction |
|------------------|------------------------------|-------------|
| **Standard Advisor** | **0.25% - 0.5%** | Monthly call + Ad-hoc intro |
| **Heavy Lifter** | **0.5% - 1.0%** | Bi-weekly calls + Protocol Design |
| **Cash Stipend?** | Only for specific deliverables (e.g., $150/hr for writing a whitepaper). Otherwise, stick to equity. |

## 3. Outreach Templates

### Template A: For Directors (Focus: Safety/Scale)
**Subject:** Quick Q on AI liability (building for [University Goal])

"Hi Dr. [Name],

I'm building GentleQuest, an AI companion specifically designed to handle the **'2 AM void'** when counseling centers are closed.

We are designing our **Crisis Escalation Protocol** (C-SSRS integration) and I'd love your eyes on it to ensure we are truly 'counselor-safe'.

I'm not selling anything. I am looking for a clinical advisor ($[Equity]% equity) to keep us honest and safe. Would you be open to a 15-min feedback chat?

Best,
[Founder]"

### Template B: For Researchers (Focus: Data/Publication)
**Subject:** Data partnership / Advisor role

"Hi Dr. [Name],

I've been following your work on [Specific Paper Topic]. I'm building GentleQuest (AI + CBT for students), and we are launching a pilot with [Number] students next month.

We have a unique dataset (real-time mood logs + intervention outcomes) that might be valuable for your research.

We are looking for a Scientific Advisor (Equity grant) to help structure our data collection for future publication. Open to a brief chat?

Best,
[Founder]"

## 4. Next Steps for Founder
1.  **List Building:** Identify 10 University Directors + 10 Digital Health Researchers on LinkedIn.
2.  **Outreach:** Send 5 messages/day using above templates.
3.  **Vetting:** During the first call, ask: *"If a student mentions suicide at 2 AM, what is the 'perfect' app behavior in your opinion?"* (Test for pragmatism vs. impossible standards).
