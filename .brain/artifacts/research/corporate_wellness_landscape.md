# Corporate Wellness Landscape: The Generic Giant Gap

**Goal:** Position GentleQuest to win B2B contracts by solving the specific failure points of "General Mental Health" (GMH) platforms.

---

## 1. Competitive SWOT (B2B Rivals)

| Competitor | Strength | The "GentleQuest" Gap (Weakness) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lyra Health** | High clinical quality; enterprise-scale. | **Expensive & Slow:** Requires therapist matching. Can feel like "more work" for an overwhelmed employee. |
| **Modern Health** | Holistic care; Stepped-care model. | **NO Controlled Substances:** Cannot assist with ADHD meds. Generic wellness content is often ignored by neurodivergent staff. |
| **Spring Health** | 1.9x ROI; Fast access. | **Lack of Specificity:** Focuses on *stress* reduction. Fails to address *Executive Dysfunction* (getting tasks done). |

---

## 2. The "GentleQuest" B2B Wedge
We don't replace Lyra/Modern Health. We are the **"Front-End to Productivity"** for the neurodivergent 15-20% of their workforce.

### The Pitch to HR:
> "Your current EAP (Lyra) is great for crisis and therapy. But what happens at 10 AM on a Tuesday when your employee is 'stuck' and can't start their report? They don't need a therapist; they need a **Body Double**. We are the tool that keeps them in flow."

---

## 3. High-Value B2B Metrics (The Dashboard)
To win against the giants, we must report on:
*   **Task Resumption Rate:** % of users who completed a stalled task after using an "AI Body Double."
*   **Academic/Work "Stickiness":** Reduction in perceived "Work Fear" (Cortisol) vs "Task Interest" (Dopamine).

---

## 4. Strategic Vulnerabilities (How to Beat Them)
1.  **AI Fatigue:** Big giants are slapping GPT-wrappers on everything. We position as **"Local-First, Privacy-First"**—essential for employees who don't want their workplace to "know" their mental state.
2.  **Generic Content:** Replace "Breathing Exercises" (which can frustrate ADHDers) with **"Active Quests"** (physical micro-movements).
