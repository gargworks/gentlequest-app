## 1. The "Robotic Empathy" Fatigue (Woebot/Wysa)
**Complaint:** Users feel patronized by "fake" empathy.
- *"It feels like talking to a yes-man who just enabled me."*
- *"I stopped using it because it kept saying generic things like 'I hear you' when I was spiraling."*

**GentleQuest Opportunity:**
- **Don't fake empathy.** Position Luna as a "Coach," not a "Friend."
