# B2B Pricing Strategy: GentleQuest University Pilots

**Objective:** Define a competitive pricing model for University Counseling Centers that undercuts massive competitors (Uwill, Headspace) while remaining profitable.

---

## 1. Market Benchmarks (The "Price to Beat")

| Competitor | Target Segment | Pricing Model | Estimated Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Uwill** | Universities | Contract (Flat + Usage) | **~$38.00 / student / year** (Based on Ohio State deal) |
| **Headspace** | Corporate | PEPM (Per Employee Per Month) | **$56 - $70 / employee / year** ($4.67 - $5.83 PEPM) |
| **Calm** | Corporate | PEPM | **$24 - $60 / employee / year** ($2 - $5 PEPM) |
| **Talkspace** | Universities | Fee-for-Service | **~$60 - $90 per session** (High cost/utilization) |

**Key Insight:** Universities pay *less* per head than corporations because they have massive populations (30k+ students) but lower utilization rates (expected ~5-10%).

---

## 2. GentleQuest Pricing Strategy

### The "90-Day Pilot" Offer (The Wedge)
**Goal:** Low risk, quick "Yes" from the Director.
*   **Price:** **$5,000 Flat Fee** (for up to 2,000 students).
*   **Per Student:** Equates to **$2.50 / student**.
*   **Includes:**
    *   Unlimited AI Chat & Quests.
    *   Crisis Escalation Protocol (Harm Detection).
    *   Weekly "Campus Mood" Report for the Director.

### The "Annual License" (Post-Pilot)
**Goal:** Recurring revenue, tailored to University budget cycles.
*   **Model:** Tiered Flat License (Avoid complicates PEPM counting).
*   **Tier 1 (Small Campus < 5k):** **$15,000 / year**.
*   **Tier 2 (Mid Campus 5k-15k):** **$35,000 / year** (~$2.30/student).
*   **Tier 3 (Large Campus 15k+):** **$60,000 / year** (~$4.00/student).

---

## 3. Why this wins?
1.  **Undercuts Uwill:** We are 5x-10x cheaper than Uwill's clinically-heavy model (~$38/student vs Our ~$3/student).
2.  **Solves the "2 AM Gap":** We pitch as *complementary* to Uwill/Counseling. "Uwill handles the therapy appointments. We handle the 2 AM panic attacks for 1/10th the cost."
3.  **Predictable Budget:** Directors hate "utilization-based" billing (paying more if students suffer more). Flat fee is safe.
