## Government Grants (Non-Dilutive)

### NIH SBIR/STTR — Primary Target

| Program | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **NOT-MH-24-120** | NIMH Special Interest: Digital Mental Health |
| **Valid Through** | January 6, 2027 |
| **Deadlines** | Jan 5, Apr 5, Sep 5 (recurring) |
| **Phase I** | ~$150K-$275K, 6-12 months |
| **Phase II** | ~$750K-$1.5M, 2 years |
| **Focus Areas** | mHealth, wearables, AI interventions, disparity reduction |

**Requirements:**
- US-owned small business (<500 employees)
- All work conducted in US
- Principal Investigator at least 51% employed by company
