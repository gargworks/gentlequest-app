## 1. The 2026 Organic Landscape: "Search Everyware"
By 2026, original organic reach is dead. Growth happens in three buckets:

### A. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
*   **The Shift:** Users don't click links; they read AI summaries (Gemini, Perplexity, SGE).
*   **Our Play:** We optimize for **Citations**.
    *   *Action:* Use `JSON-LD` structured data to define Luna's identity and the Nucleus Protocol.
    *   *Rule:* All blog posts must include a "TL;DR for AI" at the top to encourage direct citation.

### B. Social Search (The New Google)
*   **The Shift:** ADHD Gen Z/Alpha search TikTok/Reddit first.
*   **Our Play:** **Video Micro-Dosing.**
    *   *Action:* 15-second "Paralysis Hacks" on TikTok.
    *   *Rule:* lo-fi, authentic, human-shot video. Zero "corporate" sheen.

### C. Dark Social & Community
*   **The Shift:** Trust is built in unsearchable spaces (Discord, Slack, Private groups).
*   **Our Play:** **Open Source + Building in Public.**
    *   *Action:* Nucleus developers share the "messy" parts of building local-first AI.
