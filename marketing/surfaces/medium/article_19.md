## Teaser (100 words)
When overwhelm hits, you don't have time for a ten-minute meditation. You have time for three breaths. The three-breath reset is a portable micro-practice: breath one is a long exhale (release), breath two is a normal inhale and exhale (settle), breath three includes a shoulder drop (ground). It takes under twenty seconds. This essay breaks down the physiology of why three deliberate breaths interrupt the stress cascade, shares the exact protocol, and explains how to anchor it to a trigger — opening your laptop, ending a meeting, or checking your mood in an app.
