## Teaser (100 words)
After a hundred days of logging mood, the biggest surprise wasn't a pattern — it was how wrong my memory was. I remembered the bad weeks as longer than they were. I forgot the good Tuesdays entirely. The data corrected my narrative. This essay shares five lessons from a hundred days of mood tracking: memory is unreliable, small things correlate more than big ones, the best days are quiet, consistency matters more than intensity, and the act of tracking changed the thing being tracked. Not a how-to. A what-happened. And why you might want to try it.
