## Teaser (100 words)
The dominant digital wellness advice is nuclear: delete social media, go dumbphone, move to the woods. That works for a weekend. Then you reinstall everything. A gentler approach layers small constraints — notification windows, single-purpose devices for specific tasks, and intentional friction points — without requiring a lifestyle overhaul. This piece outlines a tiered digital wellness framework: Level 1 takes five minutes, Level 2 takes an afternoon, Level 3 is a weekend project. You pick the level that matches your current capacity. No guilt about the apps you keep.
