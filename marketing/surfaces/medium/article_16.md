## Teaser (100 words)
Most people only log their mood when something is wrong. This creates a dataset of distress — a one-sided record that makes life look worse than it is. When you review it, the bad days dominate because the good days were never recorded. This essay argues for logging good days with equal intent. Not forced gratitude — honest logging of genuinely okay moments. The result is a balanced archive you can revisit during hard weeks. We share the "good day minimum" protocol: one entry on any day rated 7 or above, no matter how busy.
