## Teaser (100 words)
"What did I learn today?" is too big for a tired brain at 10 PM. "What was one small thing that went slightly better than expected?" is the right size. Evening reflection fails when it demands productivity from a depleted mind. The best prompts are low-stakes, specific, and answerable in under thirty seconds. This piece shares the five-part evening check-in sequence used in GentleQuest's reflection mode — designed to close the mental loop of the day without turning bedtime into a performance review. Each prompt is field-tested for completion rate over a ninety-day window.
