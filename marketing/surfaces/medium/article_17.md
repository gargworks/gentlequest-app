## Teaser (100 words)
Perfectionism tells you that lowering your standards means giving up. The truth is the opposite: lowering your standards is how you keep going. A fifty-percent workout you actually do beats a hundred-percent workout you skip. A three-sentence journal entry you write beats a page you avoid. This essay reframes "lowering standards" as "right-sizing expectations to match your current capacity" — a dynamic skill, not a surrender. We share a weekly audit for identifying where your standards are too high to sustain, and a protocol for adjusting them without the guilt that usually follows.
