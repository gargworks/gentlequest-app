## Teaser (100 words)
Telling an anxious person to "just think positive" is like telling a drowning person to just breathe. The instruction is correct and impossible. Worse, it adds shame on top of the original feeling — now you're anxious and failing at positivity. This essay examines why thought suppression backfires (the ironic process theory), what actually works instead (naming, allowing, and gently redirecting), and how to build a daily practice that acknowledges difficult emotions without wallowing in them. The goal is not happiness. The goal is honesty, which turns out to be more sustainable than forced optimism.
