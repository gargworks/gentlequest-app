## Teaser (100 words)
Habit stacking — "after I brush my teeth, I'll journal" — is standard advice. But what if your routine is chaotic? Shift workers, freelancers, parents of young children: your day has no reliable anchors. This essay adapts habit stacking for unpredictable schedules using "trigger stacking" — attaching micro-habits to events rather than times. After you sit down at any desk. After you finish any phone call. After you pour any drink. The trigger is the action, not the clock. We share five field-tested trigger stacks and how to design your own in ten minutes.
