## Teaser (100 words)
Before coffee, before email, before the phone — ninety seconds. One question: how am I feeling right now? One question: what's one thing I'm approaching today? One question: what would make today slightly better? The morning check-in isn't meditation. It's not gratitude. It's a quick systems diagnostic for your mind. This piece explains the three-question morning check-in framework, why it works better than open-ended journaling in the first hour, and how to anchor it to an existing habit so you don't forget. Ninety seconds, three prompts, zero pressure.
