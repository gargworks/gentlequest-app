## Teaser (100 words)
Exciting habits get you started. Boring habits keep you going. The flashiest routines — cold plunges at dawn, two-hour deep work sessions — collapse under their own weight. Meanwhile, the unglamorous ones — a one-line journal entry, a thirty-second mood check, a single stretch — survive because they cost almost nothing. This essay makes the case for designing habits to be deliberately boring, drawing on the gap between motivation (which spikes) and friction (which persists). The most consistent people aren't the most disciplined. They're the ones who made the habit small enough to survive a bad day.
