## Teaser (100 words)
Every habit article assumes a good week. But the test of a reflection habit is whether it survives the week everything goes wrong — the deadline, the argument, the bad news. This essay shares three design principles for a reflection practice that holds during hard times: shrink the habit before stress hits, remove all optional steps, and pre-write the "bad day prompt" that requires zero creative energy. The insight: resilience isn't about pushing through. It's about making the habit so small that even your worst day can accommodate it. Design for the floor, not the ceiling.
