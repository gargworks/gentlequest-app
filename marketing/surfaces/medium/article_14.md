## Teaser (100 words)
Sometimes the problem isn't too much emotion. It's none. Emotional numbness — the flat, grey, "nothing matters" state — is common and rarely talked about. Journaling through it feels pointless because there's "nothing to write." But that's exactly when a structured prompt helps most. This essay offers five prompts designed for numb days, when open-ended questions fail. Each prompt is concrete, sensory, and low-affect: what did you eat, what did you notice, what was the temperature. The goal isn't to manufacture feeling. It's to maintain the practice until the signal returns.
