## Teaser (100 words)
The Sunday scaries aren't a character flaw — they're a predictable pattern. Your brain runs anticipatory simulations about the week ahead, and without a closing ritual, those simulations loop. The solution isn't to suppress the anxiety; it's to give it structure. A Sunday evening transition ritual — ten minutes of planning, five minutes of journaling, and one intentional choice about Monday morning — can interrupt the loop. This essay explains the anticipatory anxiety mechanism in plain language and offers a three-step ritual you can test tonight. No willpower required, just a timer.
