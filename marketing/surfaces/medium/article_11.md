## Teaser (100 words)
Your journal contains your most unfiltered thoughts. So why are they on someone else's server? Cloud-based journaling apps are convenient, but they introduce a trust problem: your innermost reflections are one data breach away from exposure. This essay makes the case for local-first journaling — entries stored on your device, encrypted, never sent to a server unless you choose. We cover the privacy tradeoffs of popular journaling apps, what "local-first" actually means, and why the future of personal software is data you control. Your thoughts should not require a login to access.
