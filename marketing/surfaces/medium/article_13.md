## Teaser (100 words)
"I feel bad" is a fog. "I feel disappointed, a little embarrassed, and tired" is a map. The practice of naming emotions with specificity — called emotional granularity — is one of the most validated findings in affective science. People who use precise emotion words regulate better, recover faster, and ruminate less. This essay explains why specificity matters, shares a three-tier emotion vocabulary from basic to nuanced, and offers a daily prompt that builds granularity over time. You don't need to feel better. You need to feel more accurately. The naming is the healing.
