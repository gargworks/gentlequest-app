## Teaser (100 words)
Productivity weekly reviews ask: what did I accomplish? Wellbeing weekly reviews ask a better question: what drained me, and what gave me energy? This essay outlines a fifteen-minute weekly review designed for mental health, not output. Five sections: mood summary from the week, energy audit (what drained, what fueled), one pattern noticed, one intention for next week, and one thing to let go of. It's not a performance review. It's a weather report for your inner life, taken once a week, that compounds into self-awareness over months. Template included.
