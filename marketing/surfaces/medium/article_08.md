## Teaser (100 words)
One mood entry tells you nothing. Thirty entries tell a story. After a month of logging, patterns emerge that are invisible in the moment: the Wednesday slump, the post-call dip, the Sunday spike. This essay walks through four common patterns that appear in 30-day mood data — the midweek valley, the social energy curve, the sleep-mood correlation, and the anticipatory anxiety wave — and what each one suggests about your weekly rhythm. The goal isn't diagnosis. It's awareness. You can't adjust what you can't see, and a month of data makes the invisible visible.
