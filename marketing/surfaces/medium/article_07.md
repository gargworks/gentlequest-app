## Teaser (100 words)
People wait to feel self-compassion before practicing it. That's backwards. Self-compassion is a behavior you repeat until the feeling catches up. The three-step protocol — acknowledge the difficulty, remind yourself struggle is universal, offer yourself the words you'd give a friend — works even when you don't believe it. This essay walks through the research on self-compassion as a trainable skill, shares a weekly practice prompt sequence, and explains why the "fake it till you feel it" approach is more effective than waiting for genuine warmth to arrive on its own.
