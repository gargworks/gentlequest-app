# Quora Answer 05

**Question:** What's the difference between sadness and depression?
**Target Quora Topic:** Depression, Mental Health
**Link Placement:** Bottom of answer, natural context

## Answer (300 words)

This is an important question, and I want to be careful answering it because I'm not a clinician.

**Sadness** is a normal human emotion. It comes in response to a specific event — a loss, a disappointment, a difficult change. It comes in waves — you feel it, it peaks, it recedes. Between waves, you can still function, enjoy things, and feel other emotions. Sadness is temporary and context-specific. It's your brain processing something that matters to you.

**Depression** is a clinical condition, not a transient emotion. Key differences:

- **Duration:** Sadness passes in hours or days. Depression persists for weeks or months.
- **Pervasiveness:** Sadness is about something. Depression colors everything — you can't enjoy things you normally would, even when nothing is wrong.
- **Physical symptoms:** Depression often includes changes in sleep, appetite, energy, and concentration that go beyond what sadness typically causes.
- **Self-worth:** Sadness doesn't usually attack your sense of self-worth. Depression often involves feelings of worthlessness or guilt that are disproportionate to circumstances.
- **Waves vs. baseline:** Sadness comes and goes. Depression is a lowered baseline that doesn't lift on its own.

If you're wondering whether what you're feeling is sadness or depression, that itself is worth paying attention to. A tool that can help is tracking your mood daily for a couple of weeks — if the numbers are consistently low (3–4 out of 10) for two weeks or more, that's information worth bringing to a professional. I use GentleQuest for this — it's a private mood-tracking app that shows trends over time. iOS, Android, web: https://gentlequest.app

But tracking is not diagnosis. Only a qualified mental health professional can diagnose depression. If you're experiencing persistent low mood, loss of interest in activities, or thoughts of self-harm, please see a professional — that's not something an app or a Quora answer can or should address.

*This is not a diagnosis. Please see a qualified professional for proper evaluation and treatment.*
