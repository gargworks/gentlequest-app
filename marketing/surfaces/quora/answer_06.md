# Quora Answer 06

**Question:** How do I deal with Sunday evening anxiety?
**Target Quora Topic:** Anxiety, Mental Health
**Link Placement:** Bottom of answer, natural context

## Answer (300 words)

Sunday evening anxiety — the "Sunday scaries" — is anticipatory anxiety. Your brain is running threat simulations about the upcoming week, and without a closing ritual for the weekend, those simulations loop. Here's a 12-minute ritual that interrupts the loop:

**Step 1: Preview the week (5 minutes).**
Open your calendar. Look at what's coming. Don't plan — just look. Identify one anchor event per day. This reduces uncertainty, which is the brain's main fuel for anxiety. When you know what's coming, the "what if" simulations slow down.

**Step 2: Close the weekend (5 minutes).**
Write one sentence that puts a period on the weekend. "This weekend I rested." "This weekend I saw friends." "This weekend I did nothing and that was enough." This creates a psychological boundary between weekend and week. Without it, the weekend bleeds into Monday with no clear ending, and your brain doesn't register that rest happened.

**Step 3: Choose one thing about Monday (2 minutes).**
Pick one thing about tomorrow that you're choosing — not dreading. "I'm choosing to have good coffee before the meeting." "I'm choosing to walk the long way." This restores agency. Anticipatory anxiety thrives on the feeling that things are happening to you. The choice reminds you that you're still making decisions.

Do this at the same time every Sunday. After three weeks, your brain recognizes the ritual as the transition signal, and the dread becomes a structured process instead of a free-floating spiral.
