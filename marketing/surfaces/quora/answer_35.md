# Quora Answer 35

**Question:** How do I deal with work anxiety on weekends?
**Target Quora Topic:** Workplace, Anxiety
**Link Placement:** Bottom of answer, natural context

## Answer (300 words)

Weekend work anxiety — the inability to disconnect because your mind keeps returning to work — is a sign that your work-life boundary is leaking. Here's how to fix it:

**1. Do a Friday closing ritual.**
Before you leave work (or close your laptop), do a 5-minute close: review what you accomplished, write down the top 3 priorities for Monday, and write one closing sentence: "This week is done." This creates a psychological boundary. Without it, your brain keeps the week "open" and processes it all weekend.

**2. The "parking lot" technique.**
When a work thought pops up on Saturday, don't engage with it and don't suppress it. Write it down in a dedicated "parking lot" — a notes file, a journal, anywhere. "Email Sarah about the report." Now it's parked. Your brain can let it go because it knows the thought is captured and will be addressed on Monday. The parking lot is not for solving — it's for storing.

**3. Create a physical transition.**
If you work from home, the lack of a commute means no physical boundary between work and not-work. Create one: a short walk after work, a change of clothes, a specific "end of day" action. The body needs a signal that work is over. Without it, the nervous system stays in work mode.

**4. Track the pattern.**
When does weekend work anxiety peak? Saturday morning? Sunday evening? After checking work email? Knowing the pattern lets you target the specific trigger. If Sunday evening is the peak, the Sunday reset ritual (preview the week, close the weekend, choose one thing about Monday) gives the anxiety a container.

**5. Don't check work email on weekends.**
This is the single highest-impact change you can make. Every time you check work email, you pull work context into your weekend. Even if you don't respond, the mere exposure activates work-mode thinking. Turn off work email notifications from Friday evening to Monday morning.
