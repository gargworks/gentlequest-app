# Quora Answer 28

**Question:** How do I know if I need therapy?
**Target Quora Topic:** Therapy, Mental Health
**Link Placement:** Bottom of answer, natural context

## Answer (300 words)

This is a common and important question. I'm not a clinician, but here are some generally recognized signs that professional support may be helpful:

**Signs that therapy could help:**
- Persistent low mood or anxiety lasting more than 2–3 weeks
- Difficulty functioning in daily life — work, relationships, self-care are suffering
- Feeling stuck in patterns you can't break on your own (rumination, avoidance, people-pleasing)
- Sleep or appetite changes that persist
- Using substances, food, or behaviors to cope with emotions
- Feeling disconnected from people or activities you used to enjoy
- Thoughts of self-harm (this requires immediate professional support)
- A major life event (loss, transition, trauma) that you're struggling to process

**Signs that self-help might be sufficient (for now):**
- Mild, situational stress that comes and goes
- You have coping strategies that work most of the time
- Your mood fluctuates but generally returns to baseline within days
- You have a support system you can talk to

**The key distinction: duration and impact.**
Everyone has bad days and bad weeks. The question is whether the difficulty is persistent (lasting weeks, not days) and whether it's impacting your ability to function. If stress is temporary and you're still showing up for your life, self-help tools may be enough. If the difficulty is persistent and your functioning is declining, that's a strong signal to seek professional support.

**A tool that can help you decide:**
Track your mood daily for 2–3 weeks. If the numbers are consistently low (below 5/10) for two weeks or more, or if you see a clear downward trend, that's objective data to bring to a professional. I use GentleQuest for this — it shows trends over time, and the data is more useful than "I've been feeling down" when talking to a clinician. iOS, Android, web: https://gentlequest.app

**You don't need to be "bad enough" to go to therapy.**
Therapy isn't only for crises. It's also for personal growth, improving relationships, and building coping skills. If you're wondering whether you need it, that curiosity itself is worth exploring with a professional.

*This is not a diagnosis. If you're experiencing persistent symptoms or thoughts of self-harm, please see a qualified professional or contact emergency services immediately.*
