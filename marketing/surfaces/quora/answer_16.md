# Quora Answer 16

**Question:** Why do I feel worse after scrolling social media?
**Target Quora Topic:** Mental Health, Social Media
**Link Placement:** Bottom of answer, natural context

## Answer (300 words)

The "feel worse after scrolling" phenomenon is well-documented and has specific mechanisms:

**1. The comparison engine.**
Your brain automatically compares your life to what you see. Social media shows you other people's highlights — vacations, achievements, happy moments — while you experience your own full spectrum including the boring and difficult parts. The comparison is rigged: their best moments vs. your average moments. You always lose.

**2. Dopamine dysregulation.**
Each scroll delivers a tiny dopamine hit — a new post, a notification, something novel. After 20 minutes of scrolling, your dopamine system is depleted, and you feel the crash: flat, irritable, unmotivated. This isn't sadness — it's the neurochemical after-effect of overstimulation.

**3. Attention fragmentation.**
Rapid scrolling trains your brain to switch attention every 3–5 seconds. After a scrolling session, your ability to focus on anything — a book, a conversation, a task — is temporarily reduced. You feel foggy and restless without knowing why.

**4. Emotional residue.**
You absorb emotions from content. Even if you don't engage, your brain processes the outrage, the envy, the anxiety in the posts you see. You carry that emotional residue with you after you close the app.

**What to do:**
- Set a timer before opening social media. 10 minutes. When it goes off, close the app.
- Move social apps off your home screen. Add friction to opening them.
- After scrolling, do a "reset" — three deliberate breaths, or a one-sentence journal entry about how you actually feel right now. This breaks the passive absorption and re-engages your own internal state.
