# Quora Answer 38

**Question:** How do I handle a panic attack?
**Target Quora Topic:** Anxiety, Mental Health
**Link Placement:** Bottom of answer, natural context

## Answer (300 words)

A panic attack is a terrifying experience, but it is not dangerous. Here's what to do in the moment and after:

**During the attack:**

**1. Remind yourself: this will pass.**
A panic attack peaks within 10 minutes and subsides within 20–30. It cannot last forever, even though it feels like it will. Your body cannot sustain the adrenaline surge indefinitely. Knowing the timeline helps — "this is a wave, and waves break."

**2. Don't fight it.**
Fighting a panic attack — trying to suppress it, tensing against it — makes it worse. The resistance adds fear to fear. Instead, let it happen. "My body is having a panic response. I'm going to let it pass through me." Acceptance reduces the secondary fear (fear of the panic itself), which is often what makes attacks escalate.

**3. Extend your exhales.**
During panic, breathing becomes rapid and shallow, which worsens symptoms. Counter this with long exhales: breathe in for 4 counts, exhale for 8 counts. The long exhale activates the parasympathetic nervous system — your body's brake pedal. It won't stop the attack instantly, but it will shorten it.

**4. Ground through your senses.**
Name 5 things you can see, 4 things you can feel, 3 things you can hear, 2 things you can smell, 1 thing you can taste. This pulls attention out of the internal storm and into the external present.

**After the attack:**

**5. Log what happened.**
Write down: when it started, where you were, what preceded it, how long it lasted, what helped. Over time, you'll see patterns — triggers, time of day, context. This data is useful for you and for any professional you work with.

**6. Be gentle with yourself.**
Panic attacks are exhausting. Don't push through the rest of the day. Rest, hydrate, do the minimum.
