# Quora Answer 04

**Question:** How do I start journaling when I hate writing?
**Target Quora Topic:** Journaling, Self Improvement
**Link Placement:** Bottom of answer, natural context

## Answer (300 words)

Most people who "hate writing" don't hate journaling — they hate the version of journaling they've been sold. The three-page morning pages routine. The beautifully handwritten gratitude list. The deep, introspective essay. That's not journaling. That's performance.

Real journaling can be one sentence. One word. One number. Here's how to start if you hate writing:

**Step 1: Lower the bar until it's underground.**
Your journal entry should be so small that skipping it feels stranger than doing it. One sentence about your day. One mood rating. One thing you noticed. If that feels like too much, one word. "Tired." That's a complete entry.

**Step 2: Use prompts, not blank pages.**
A blank page is the enemy of a reluctant writer. Prompts remove the decision fatigue. "What was one small thing that went better than expected today?" is easier to answer than "Write about your day." Specific questions get specific — and short — answers.

**Step 3: Make it a behavior, not an art.**
Don't worry about good writing. Don't worry about complete sentences. Don't worry about being interesting. The journal is for you. It doesn't need to be readable, coherent, or grammatical. It needs to exist.

**Step 4: Anchor it to something you already do.**
After you brush your teeth. After you pour coffee. After you plug in your phone at night. Attach the one-sentence entry to an existing habit and let the anchor pull it along.

**Step 5: Use an app if paper feels like too much pressure.**
A notebook feels permanent. An app feels lighter — type one sentence, close it, done. The goal isn't to become a writer. The goal is to have a quiet record of your life that you can look back on. One sentence at a time is enough. One word is enough. Starting is the only hard part, and starting takes one sentence.
