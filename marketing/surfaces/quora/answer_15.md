# Quora Answer 15

**Question:** How do I manage stress at work?
**Target Quora Topic:** Stress Management, Workplace
**Link Placement:** Bottom of answer, natural context

## Answer (300 words)

Workplace stress is rarely about the workload alone — it's about the lack of recovery intervals. Here's a practical system:

**1. Insert micro-recovery breaks.**
Your brain isn't designed for 8 hours of continuous focus. Every 90–120 minutes, take a 5-minute break. Not a "check your phone" break — a real break. Walk, stretch, look out a window, do the three-breath reset (one long exhale, one normal breath, one shoulder drop). These micro-recoveries prevent the cortisol accumulation that turns a manageable day into an overwhelming one.

**2. Do an energy audit weekly.**
Once a week, ask: what drained me? What fueled me? One of each. You'll start seeing patterns — maybe the Tuesday meeting drains you every week, or the Wednesday lunch with a specific colleague fuels you. You can't reduce what drained you if you don't know what it was. This takes 3 minutes and changes how you structure your weeks.

**3. Close the mental loop at the end of the day.**
Before you leave (or close your laptop at home), write one sentence: "Today I did ___ and tomorrow I'm starting with ___." This creates a boundary between work and non-work. Without it, the work follows you home in the form of background processing — the thoughts that pop up at 9 PM about something you forgot to do.

**4. Stop measuring yourself by output alone.**
If your only metric is "how much did I get done," you'll optimize for quantity and burn out. Add a second metric: "how was my energy at the end of the day?" If you're consistently finishing at a 2/5, something in the system is unsustainable, and no amount of pushing will fix it.

I track daily energy and mood using GentleQuest — a private app that shows trends over weeks so I can see which work patterns are sustainable and which aren't. It's local-first, so work stress notes stay on my device. iOS, Android, web: https://gentlequest.app

The goal isn't to eliminate stress — some stress is unavoidable and even useful. The goal is to build recovery into the system so stress doesn't accumulate into burnout.

*This is not a diagnosis. If workplace stress is affecting your health, sleep, or relationships, please see a qualified professional.*
