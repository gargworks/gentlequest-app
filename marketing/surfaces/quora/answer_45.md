# Quora Answer 45

**Question:** How do I build resilience?
**Target Quora Topic:** Mental Health, Self Improvement
**Link Placement:** Bottom of answer, natural context

## Answer (300 words)

Resilience isn't about being tough — it's about having the right tools to recover from difficulty. Here's how to build it:

**1. Build a recovery routine, not just a coping routine.**
Most people have coping strategies (things they do during stress) but no recovery strategies (things they do after stress to reset). Coping gets you through the moment; recovery rebuilds the buffer. Recovery includes: sleep, genuine downtime, physical movement, time in nature, and connection with people you trust. Without recovery, coping strategies become exhaustion management.

**2. Practice the "name it to tame it" technique.**
When something difficult happens, name the emotion before trying to fix the situation. "I'm feeling overwhelmed and scared." The naming engages the prefrontal cortex and reduces amygdala activation, giving you more cognitive control over your response. You can't manage what you can't identify.

**3. Keep a "survived it" record.**
Resilience is partly confidence — the belief that you can handle what comes. That confidence comes from evidence. Keep a record of difficult things you've survived: "Last year I thought the project would break me. It didn't." When a new challenge arrives, the record reminds you that you've been here before and gotten through.

**4. Maintain the floor during hard times.**
Resilience isn't about maintaining your full routine during a crisis. It's about maintaining the floor — the minimum viable version of self-care. One meal. One glass of water. One sentence in a journal. The floor keeps the structure intact until the crisis passes and you can rebuild.

**5. Build a support network before you need it.**
Resilience is not a solo activity. The most resilient people have strong connections they can lean on. Build those connections during good times, not during crises. One text a day to one person maintains the network so it's there when you need it.

**6. Track your recovery.**
After a hard period, track your mood and energy daily. Watch the trend — is it going up? Staying flat? The data tells you whether you're recovering or still depleted.
