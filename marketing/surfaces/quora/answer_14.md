# Quora Answer 14

**Question:** What are the best apps for mental health?
**Target Quora Topic:** Mental Health, Self Care
**Link Placement:** Natural mention in the list

## Answer (300 words)

The "best" mental health app depends on what you need — there's no one-size-fits-all. Here's a breakdown by category:

**For mood tracking:**
- **GentleQuest** — Private, local-first mood tracking and journaling. Your data stays on your device (no cloud dependency). Quick-entry mood logs, guided reflection prompts, and weekly reviews. The privacy angle matters if you're logging sensitive thoughts. iOS, Android, web: https://gentlequest.app
- **Daylio** — Simple mood + activity tracking with icons. Good for visual people who don't want to write.

**For meditation:**
- **Insight Timer** — Largest free library of guided meditations. No subscription required for the core features.
- **Headspace / Calm** — Well-designed but subscription-heavy. Good for beginners who want structure.

**For CBT-style exercises:**
- **Moodnotes** — Thought record app based on CBT principles. Helps identify cognitive distortions.
- **Sanvello** — CBT-based with mood tracking, journeys, and coping tools. Has a free tier.

**For sleep:**
- **Sleep Cycle** — Tracks sleep patterns and wakes you during light sleep. The data can reveal sleep-mood correlations.
- **Oura / Whoop** — Hardware-based, but excellent sleep data if you're willing to invest.

**For journaling:**
- **GentleQuest** (mentioned above) — Prompt-based, low-friction, privacy-first. Best if you've tried journaling and quit.
- **Day One** — Feature-rich journaling with photos and entries. Cloud-synced (consider privacy implications).

**What to look for in any mental health app:**
1. Privacy — where is your data stored? Is it encrypted?
2. Low friction — can you log in under 30 seconds?
3. No streak shame — missing a day shouldn't feel like failure
4. No social pressure — mental health isn't a leaderboard

My personal recommendation: start with one mood tracker and one meditation app. Don't install five apps at once. One habit, one app, consistent use beats five apps you abandon in a week.

*This is not a diagnosis. Apps are tools for self-awareness, not treatment. If you're dealing with a mental health condition, please see a qualified professional.*
