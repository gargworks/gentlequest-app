# Quora Answer 40

**Question:** What are the signs of emotional burnout?
**Target Quora Topic:** Burnout, Mental Health
**Link Placement:** Bottom of answer, natural context

## Answer (300 words)

Emotional burnout sneaks up gradually — you don't notice it until you're deep in it. Here are the signs to watch for:

**Emotional signs:**
- Emotional numbness or flatness — things that used to bring joy don't anymore
- Increased irritability — small things trigger disproportionate frustration
- Cynicism or detachment — feeling "over it" toward work, relationships, or activities you used to care about
- Feeling ineffective — no matter how much you do, it doesn't feel like enough
- Dread — anticipating the day, the week, the meeting with a sense of heaviness

**Physical signs:**
- Persistent exhaustion that sleep doesn't fix
- Changes in sleep patterns — trouble falling asleep or sleeping too much
- Changes in appetite — eating more or less than usual
- Frequent headaches, muscle tension, or gastrointestinal issues
- Getting sick more often — burnout suppresses immune function

**Cognitive signs:**
- Difficulty concentrating — tasks that used to be easy feel overwhelming
- Brain fog — forgetting things, losing train of thought
- Reduced creativity — problem-solving feels harder than usual
- Procrastination — avoiding tasks not out of laziness but out of depletion

**Behavioral signs:**
- Withdrawing from social activities — not out of preference but out of exhaustion
- Increased use of substances, food, or screens to cope
- Neglecting self-care — skipping exercise, meals, or hygiene
- Working more but accomplishing less — the effort-to-output ratio declines

**What to do:**
If you recognize 3 or more of these signs persisting for more than a couple of weeks, take it seriously. Burnout doesn't resolve by pushing harder — it resolves by reducing load and increasing recovery.

Track your energy daily for 2 weeks. If it's consistently low despite rest, that's data — not weakness. I use GentleQuest to track both mood and energy, and the trends make burnout visible before it becomes a crisis. iOS, Android, web: https://gentlequest.app

*This is not a diagnosis. Burnout can overlap with depression and anxiety. If symptoms persist or worsen, please see a qualified professional.*
