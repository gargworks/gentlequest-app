# Quora Answer 39

**Question:** How do I stop doomscrolling?
**Target Quora Topic:** Digital Wellness, Mental Health
**Link Placement:** Bottom of answer, natural context

## Answer (300 words)

Doomscrolling — compulsively scrolling negative news or social media — is a stress-response behavior, not a character flaw. Here's how to break the loop:

**1. Understand why you do it.**
Doomscrolling is your brain's attempt to gather information about threats. It thinks: "If I can find all the bad news, I can prepare for it." The problem: there's infinite bad news, so the scanning never terminates. You're not addicted to negativity — you're stuck in a threat-scanning loop that has no natural endpoint.

**2. Add friction to the scroll.**
The easier it is to scroll, the harder it is to stop. Add friction:
- Move news and social apps off your home screen.
- Set app limits (1 hour/day for your biggest time-sink app).
- Use grayscale mode — the lack of color makes scrolling less rewarding.
- Put your phone in another room when you're working or relaxing.

**3. Set a "scroll timer."**
Before you open a news or social app, set a timer for 10 minutes. When it goes off, close the app. Not "check one more thing" — close it. The timer creates an external boundary that your internal willpower doesn't have to enforce.

**4. Replace the behavior, don't just remove it.**
If you stop doomscrolling without replacing the behavior, you'll fill the time with something else — usually another phone-based habit. Have a replacement ready: a book, a sketchbook, a journaling app, a walk. The replacement needs to be as accessible as the phone.

**5. Do a post-scroll reset.**
After a scrolling session, do a 60-second reset: log your mood, write one sentence about how you feel. This breaks the passive absorption and re-engages your own internal state. The data over weeks is eye-opening — you'll see the mood dip that follows doomscrolling sessions.

I use GentleQuest for the post-scroll reset — a quick mood log and one-sentence entry. After tracking this for a few weeks, the correlation between doomscrolling and low mood became undeniable, which made it easier to stop. iOS, Android, web: https://gentlequest.app

*This is not a diagnosis. If doomscrolling is significantly affecting your mental health, please see a qualified professional.*
