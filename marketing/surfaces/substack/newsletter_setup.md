# GentleQuest Substack Newsletter Setup

## Newsletter Name
**The Gentle Quest**

## Tagline
One small experiment in wellbeing, every week.

## Description (Substack "About" page)
The Gentle Quest is a weekly newsletter for people who want to practice mental wellness in small, repeatable ways. No life overhauls. No toxic positivity. Just one idea, one prompt, and one small experiment you can try before the next issue arrives. Each week covers a theme — mood tracking, journaling, reflection, habit design, self-compassion — with a 600-word essay, a practical prompt, and a link to try it in the GentleQuest app.

## Frequency
Weekly. One issue per week, sent on Sunday at 8 AM local time. The Sunday send is intentional: readers receive the prompt at the start of their week, when they have time to reflect and set up a small experiment.

## Audience
- Primary: Adults aged 25–45 who are interested in mental wellness but skeptical of wellness influencers
- Secondary: People who have tried journaling, mood tracking, or meditation and quit
- Tertiary: Productivity enthusiasts looking to apply structured frameworks to emotional wellbeing
- Tone: Warm, practical, low-pressure. No urgency language. No shame. No "you must."

## Publication Settings
1. Create publication at substack.com — name: "The Gentle Quest"
2. Set tagline: "One small experiment in wellbeing, every week."
3. Upload a neutral logo (soft gradient, no faces, no names)
4. Configure custom domain if available (e.g., newsletter.gentlequest.app)
5. Set publish day: Sunday
6. Set publish time: 8:00 AM
7. Enable free subscription (no paywall for the first 20 issues)
8. Connect to Twitter/X for auto-share on publish
9. Set welcome email: "Welcome to The Gentle Quest. Here's your first experiment: log one mood rating today. That's it. See you Sunday."

## Issue Template Structure
Each issue follows this format:
1. **Issue number and theme** (header)
2. **Headline** (one line, conversational)
3. **Body** (~600 words, essay-style, one core idea)
4. **This Week's Experiment** (one actionable prompt)
5. **CTA** (link to try in GentleQuest)
6. **Image brief** (description of the header image)
7. **Publish date placeholder**
