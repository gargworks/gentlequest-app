### Description

GentleQuest is an independently built journaling and mood-tracking app, created and maintained by a solo developer. It represents a specific philosophy of indie product building:

**Indie product principles:**
- **Solve a real problem you have:** Built to scratch a personal itch — the need for a private, low-friction journaling tool that doesn't compromise on privacy.
- **Sustainable over scalable:** Not designed for venture-scale growth. Designed to be useful, sustainable, and respectful of users.
- **Privacy as a feature, not a tradeoff:** No data harvesting, no ad SDKs, no engagement metrics. The business model doesn't conflict with user wellbeing.
- **Cross-platform from day one:** iOS, Android, and web — because users shouldn't have to switch devices to maintain their journaling practice.
- **Free core features:** The essential functionality — mood logging, journaling, reflection prompts — is free. No paywall on basic mental health tools.

**Product details:**
- Mood tracking with trend visualization
- Structured reflection prompts (morning, evening, weekly)
- Self-compassion guided protocol
- Local-first, encrypted, on-device storage
- No account required, no cloud dependency

### Why it fits this list

awesome-indie showcases products and resources from independent makers. GentleQuest is relevant because:

1. **Indie-built:** Solo developer, no funding, no team — built and shipped independently.
2. **Real product with real users:** Live on App Store, Play Store, and web. Not a side project — a shipped product.
3. **Anti-pattern in the best way:** In a market full of venture-backed wellness apps with engagement metrics and subscription walls, GentleQuest demonstrates that indie products can compete on principles (privacy, simplicity, respect for users).
4. **Cross-platform shipping:** Demonstrates that a solo developer can ship to three platforms — relevant to other indie makers considering their platform strategy.
