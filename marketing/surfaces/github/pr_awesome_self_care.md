### Description

GentleQuest is a self-care app built around the principle that small, consistent practices outperform large, unsustainable ones. It provides:

**Daily self-care practices:**
- **Morning check-in:** Three questions in 90 seconds — how am I feeling, what am I approaching, what would make today slightly better.
- **Evening reflection:** Five low-friction prompts designed for a tired brain, including a "let go of" release prompt.
- **Mood tracking:** One-tap mood rating that builds into a trend chart over weeks.
- **Weekly review:** 15-minute wellbeing review covering mood summary, energy audit, pattern noticed, intention, and release.

**Self-care design principles:**
- **No streak shame:** Missing a day doesn't reset anything. Guilt-free gaps.
- **Floor-level habits:** Every practice has a minimum viable version that survives bad days.
- **Privacy as self-care:** Your reflections stay on your device. No cloud, no account, no audience. Honesty requires safety.
- **Evidence-informed:** Implements practices from self-compassion research (Neff), expressive writing (Pennebaker), and implementation intentions (Gollwitzer).

### Why it fits this list

awesome-self-care curates tools and resources for personal wellbeing. GentleQuest is a natural fit because:

1. **Purpose-built for self-care:** Not a productivity app repurposed for wellness — designed from the ground up for daily mental health maintenance.
2. **Sustainable design:** The app's philosophy — small habits, no guilt, floor-level practices — aligns with the self-care principle of sustainability over intensity.
3. **Privacy-respecting:** Self-care requires a safe space. Local-first storage ensures users can be fully honest in their reflections.
4. **Free and accessible:** No paywall on core self-care features. Available on all platforms.
