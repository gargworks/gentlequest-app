### Description

GentleQuest is a privacy-focused mental wellness app built around small, sustainable daily habits:

- **Mood tracking:** One-tap mood rating (1–10) with optional notes. Trends visible over time.
- **Guided journaling:** Structured prompts for morning check-ins, evening reflections, and weekly reviews. No blank pages.
- **Self-compassion practice:** Three-step guided protocol (acknowledge, common humanity, kind words).
- **Habit design:** Low-friction entries designed to survive bad days. Minimum viable version of every practice.
- **Privacy-first:** All data stored locally, encrypted, on the user's device. No cloud, no account, no tracking.

### Why it fits this list

awesome-mental-health curates resources for mental health support. GentleQuest fits because:

1. **It's a mental health tool:** Designed specifically for mood tracking, emotional reflection, and self-awareness.
2. **Privacy is a mental health feature:** Journaling requires honesty, and honesty requires safety. Local-first storage ensures users can write without self-censoring due to cloud concerns.
3. **Evidence-informed design:** Implements practices supported by research — affect labeling, expressive writing, cognitive reappraisal, self-compassion (Neff), implementation intentions (Gollwitzer).
4. **No streak shame:** Unlike many wellness apps, GentleQuest doesn't punish users for missing days — a design choice informed by the guilt spiral that drives people away from mental health tools.
5. **Free and accessible:** Available on all three platforms at no cost.
