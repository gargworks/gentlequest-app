# Video 04 — YouTube Long-Form Brief

**Title:** I Tried the "Boring Habits" Approach for 30 Days
**Target Audience:** Self-improvement enthusiasts who have tried dramatic habit overhauls and burned out. People interested in sustainable, low-friction routines.

## Script Outline (7 minutes)

### Hook (0:00–0:45)
"I deleted my 5 AM cold plunge routine. I cancelled my two-hour deep work blocks. I replaced them with the most boring habits I could design. Thirty days later, I'd done more than any intense routine ever gave me."

### Act 1: The Problem with Exciting Habits (0:45–2:30)
- Exciting habits are motivation-dependent — motivation spikes then crashes
- The "new routine high" lasts 1–2 weeks, then reality hits
- Dramatic habits require perfect conditions — one bad day derails everything
- The social media factor: we design habits for Instagram, not for survival

### Act 2: The Boring Habit Experiment (2:30–5:00)
- Week 1: One sentence journal entry (took 45 seconds)
- Week 2: Added a mood check (took 15 seconds)
- Week 3: Added one stretch (took 30 seconds)
- Week 4: Added one evening reflection prompt (took 60 seconds)
- Total daily time: under 2.5 minutes
- What happened: 30/30 days completed. Zero skips. Zero guilt.
- Show the data: app streak view, 30 checkmarks

### Act 3: Why Boring Wins (5:00–6:15)
- Boring habits survive bad days, travel days, sick days
- The compounding effect: 30 tiny entries > 3 perfect pages
- Identity shift: "I'm someone who shows up daily" beats "I'm someone with an impressive routine"
- The real metric: not intensity, but survival rate across a month

### Outro / CTA (6:15–7:00)
- "GentleQuest is built around boring habits. Tiny prompts, no streak shame, no pressure. Links below."
- Platform links
- "This is not a diagnosis. Please see a qualified professional for clinical concerns."
- "Subscribe for more quiet experiments."
