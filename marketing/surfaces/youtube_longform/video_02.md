# Video 02 — YouTube Long-Form Brief

**Title:** The 2-Minute Journal Method That Actually Sticks
**Target Audience:** People who have tried journaling and quit multiple times. Busy professionals, students, and parents who want reflection without the time commitment.

## Script Outline (6 minutes)

### Hook (0:00–0:40)
"I've started journaling eleven times. I've quit eleven times. Then I changed one rule — the entry had to take under two minutes — and I haven't missed a day in six months. Here's the method."

### Act 1: Why Journaling Fails (0:40–2:00)
- The blank page problem: too much freedom = too much friction
- The perfectionism trap: entries feel like they need to be "good"
- The time problem: ten minutes feels doable on day one, impossible by day seven
- The consistency cliff: most people quit between days 4 and 10

### Act 2: The 2-Minute Method (2:00–4:00)
- Rule 1: One sentence about your day
- Rule 2: One word for your mood
- Rule 3: One thing you noticed
- That's it. Three lines. Under two minutes.
- Why it works: the barrier is so low that skipping feels harder than doing it
- Show the app: screen recording of completing a full entry in 90 seconds

### Act 3: How to Never Miss a Day (4:00–5:15)
- Anchor it to an existing habit (after brushing teeth, after coffee)
- Use a prompt rotation so you never face a blank page
- Track your streak — not for pressure, for visibility
- The "bad day entry": on hard days, write "today was hard" and that counts

### Outro / CTA (5:15–6:00)
- "If you want to try this, I built GentleQuest around this exact method. Free, private, no streak shame."
- Platform links on screen
- "This is not a diagnosis. Please see a qualified professional for clinical concerns."
- "Subscribe for more small habit experiments."
