# Video 08 — YouTube Long-Form Brief

**Title:** The Privacy Argument for Local-Only Journaling Apps
**Target Audience:** Privacy-conscious users, tech-savvy individuals, and anyone who has reconsidered what they write in a cloud-based journal after a data breach news story.

## Script Outline (7 minutes)

### Hook (0:00–0:45)
"Your journal contains the thoughts you wouldn't say out loud. So why are they stored on a server you don't control? Let's talk about why local-only journaling matters — and why most journaling apps get this wrong."

### Act 1: The Privacy Problem (0:45–3:00)
- What cloud journaling actually means: your entries live on someone else's computer
- The data breach reality: journaling apps have been breached before
- The terms of service problem: many apps claim the right to analyze your entries
- The AI training concern: some apps explicitly use journal content for model training
- The login problem: you need internet access to read your own thoughts
- The export problem: if the app shuts down, your entries may be gone

### Act 2: What Local-First Means (3:00–5:00)
- Your entries are stored on your device, encrypted, by default
- No server required to read or write
- Sync is optional and end-to-end encrypted — you choose if and when
- You can export your data at any time, in a standard format
- No account required, no email needed, no tracking
- Show app: screen recording of GentleQuest working offline, then exporting entries

### Act 3: The Tradeoffs and How to Evaluate (5:00–6:15)
- Tradeoff 1: No multi-device sync without some setup (acceptable for most)
- Tradeoff 2: You're responsible for backups (but the app can automate this)
- Tradeoff 3: Less "social" features — no sharing, no community (this is a feature, not a bug)
- How to evaluate any journaling app's privacy: check where data is stored, check the ToS, check if there's an offline mode

### Outro / CTA (6:15–7:00)
- "GentleQuest is local-first by design. Your entries stay on your device. Links below."
- Platform links
- "This is not a diagnosis. Please see a qualified professional for clinical concerns."
