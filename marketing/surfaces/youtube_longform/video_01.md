# Video 01 — YouTube Long-Form Brief

**Title:** I Tracked My Mood for 100 Days. Here's What Actually Happened.
**Target Audience:** People aged 22–40 interested in self-improvement, mental wellness, and data-driven personal growth. Skeptical of wellness influencers, drawn to honest experiments.

## Script Outline (7 minutes)

### Hook (0:00–0:45)
"I tracked my mood every single day for 100 days. Not with a fancy wearable. Just a number from 1 to 10 and one sentence. What I found wasn't what I expected — and it changed how I think about my own memory."

### Act 1: The Setup (0:45–2:00)
- Why I started: kept forgetting whether last week was good or bad
- The method: one mood rating, one sentence, same time each evening
- The tool: a simple app (show screen recording of GentleQuest mood entry)
- The commitment: 100 days, no skipping, no judging the number

### Act 2: What the Data Showed (2:00–4:30)
- My memory was wrong — bad weeks felt longer than they were
- Tuesdays were consistently my best day (show chart)
- Social events correlated with next-day dips, not spikes
- The "quiet good days" — days I rated 8 but would have called unremarkable
- The tracking itself changed my awareness — I noticed mood shifts in real-time

### Act 3: Three Takeaways (4:30–6:00)
1. Your memory is not a reliable narrator — data corrects the story
2. Small patterns matter more than big events
3. Consistency beats intensity — the value is in the archive, not any single entry

### Outro / CTA (6:00–7:00)
- "If you want to try this, you don't need anything fancy. I use GentleQuest — it's free, private, and the mood log takes about ten seconds."
- Show all three platform links on screen
- "This is not a diagnosis. If you're struggling, please see a qualified professional."
- "Subscribe for more quiet experiments. No hype, just data."
