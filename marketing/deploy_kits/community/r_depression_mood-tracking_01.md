## Post
- **Title:** Does anyone else find mood tracking exhausting? What actually worked for you?
- **Body:**

I've tried mood tracking a bunch of times and usually quit within a week. The problem isn't the concept — it's that most apps make it feel like homework. Streaks, notifications, "you haven't checked in today!" guilt. When you're depressed, the last thing you need is an app scolding you.

What finally stuck for me was the opposite approach: no streaks, no push notifications, and I only log when I actually feel like it. Some days that's once, some days it's zero, some days it's three times. The point isn't a perfect dataset — it's just catching enough snapshots to see the shape of things over time.

After about a month of this low-pressure version, I could actually see that my worst days tended to follow nights where I slept badly, and that my mood in the first hour after waking was usually worse than it was by evening. That's not earth-shattering but it gave me something concrete to work with instead of the vague sense that everything is always terrible.

Curious if others here have found a tracking approach that doesn't feel like another obligation.
