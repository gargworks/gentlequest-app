## Post
- **Title:** A list of actually-free mental health resources that don't have a paywall trap
- **Body:**

I got frustrated by how many "free" mental health resources are actually free for 3 days or free to download but $14.99/month to actually use. So I started keeping a list of things that are genuinely free with no catch. Sharing in case it helps someone.

- **7 Cups** — free peer listening (paid option for professional therapy)
- **CBT Thought Record apps** — several genuinely free ones exist
- **Your local library** — tons of CBT and mindfulness workbooks, free with a card
- **Crisis Text Line** — text HOME to 741741 (US), free 24/7
- **SAMhsa National Helpline** — 1-800-662-4357, free treatment referral
- **A local-storage journaling app** — I use one that's free with no premium tier, no ads, just a private place to write and do some basic CBT exercises

The thing I look for now: does it say "free" on the download page and then immediately ask for a credit card? If yes, it's not free. Genuinely free tools exist, they're just less aggressively marketed because nobody's making money off them.

What would you add to this list?
