## Post
- **Title:** Looking for a truly private journaling app — no cloud, no account, no AI reading my entries
- **Body:**

I've been journaling for years and recently got spooked by how many journaling apps sync everything to the cloud by default. My journal has my most honest thoughts — stuff I wouldn't say to anyone. The idea that it's sitting on a server, potentially accessible to employees or future acquirers or whoever, makes me write less honestly.

I went looking for something that's genuinely local-only. No account. No cloud sync. No "we use your entries to improve our AI features." Just a private notebook on my device that I could export if I wanted to.

Found one that fits this and it's changed how I write. I'm more honest now because I'm not subconsciously editing for an audience I can't see. The journal feels like mine again.

Curious — does anyone else here care about this? Most people I mention it to seem fine with cloud sync, which surprises me given how personal journaling is.
