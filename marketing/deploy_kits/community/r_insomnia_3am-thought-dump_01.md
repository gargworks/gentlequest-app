## Post
- **Title:** The 3am thought dump technique that helps me get back to sleep
- **Body:**

We all know the 3am wake-up. You're suddenly wide awake and your brain decides this is the perfect time to: replay that embarrassing thing from 2019, list everything you need to do tomorrow, worry about something you can't control, and solve problems that don't exist yet.

I used to lie there trying to force sleep, which never works, or scroll my phone, which makes it worse. The thing that's helped most: I keep my phone within reach with a journaling app open to a blank entry. When I wake up at 3am with a racing mind, I dump everything into the note. Every thought, every worry, every to-do. No structure, no coherence, just get it out.

Then I put the phone down. And usually — not always, but usually — my brain lets go because the thoughts are "saved." It's like my mind was keeping me awake to make sure I wouldn't forget these things, and writing them down satisfies that need.

The rules I follow for this to work:
- Dim screen all the way down
- No reading old entries, just write new ones
- No checking the time after I put the phone down
- If I'm not asleep in 20 minutes, I write more

It's not perfect but it's turned nights where I'd be awake for 2 hours into nights where I'm back asleep in 20 minutes. Enough of a difference that I wanted to share.
