## Post
- **Title:** Grounding techniques for social situations — the ones I can actually do in public
- **Body:**

Most grounding techniques are designed for when you're alone or in a safe space. Close your eyes, do a body scan, breathe deeply for 5 minutes. Great — except I need grounding in meetings, at parties, in conversations. I can't close my eyes and do a 5-minute breathing exercise while someone is talking to me.

Here's what I've found works in actual social situations:

- **Feel my feet**: Press my toes into the floor inside my shoes. Nobody can see it. It brings me back into my body without anyone knowing.
- **Texture**: Touch something with a distinct texture — the edge of my sleeve, a key in my pocket, the arm of a chair. Subtle but grounding.
- **Name 3 things I can see**: Not out loud. Just internally notice three objects in the room. It pulls me out of my internal spiral and back into the environment.
- **Slow exhale**: I can't do a big breathing exercise but I can make my next exhale longer than my inhale, once or twice. Enough to settle without looking weird.

I keep these written down on my phone so I can glance at them before I walk into a situation. The reminder helps because in the moment my brain defaults to "panic" not "use technique."

What works for you in public situations?
