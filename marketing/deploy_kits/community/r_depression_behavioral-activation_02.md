## Post
- **Title:** Behavioral activation actually works but it's hard to start — here's what helped me
- **Body:**

My therapist suggested behavioral activation a while back and my honest first reaction was "great, another thing I'm failing at." The idea — do small activities even when you don't feel like it, because action can precede motivation — makes sense intellectually. But when you're flattened, "just go for a walk" feels like being told to climb Everest.

What made it click for me was making the steps genuinely tiny and writing them down ahead of time. Not "exercise" but "walk to the end of the block." Not "clean the house" but "put three things in the trash." The bar was almost embarrassingly low.

I'd write down one or two of these the night before, and then just check them off the next day without judging whether they "counted." Some days I did more, some days I only did the one tiny thing. But having it written down meant I didn't have to negotiate with myself in the moment, which is when depression always wins.

Over a few weeks the cumulative effect was real. Not cured — but less stuck. Has anyone else tried this approach? What did your "tiny enough to actually do it" steps look like?
