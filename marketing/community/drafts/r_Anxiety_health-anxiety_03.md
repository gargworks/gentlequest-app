Honest question for fellow health anxiety folks. I go through cycles where I become hyper-aware of every sensation in my body and spiral from there. A weird heartbeat, a twitch, a headache — suddenly I'm convinced it's something serious.

I've seen people suggest tracking symptoms to "see they're not new." But I worry that tracking might just feed the hypervigilance. Like, if I'm writing down every body sensation, am I just training myself to notice more of them?

I tried it cautiously for two weeks — logging only when I felt calm, not in the moment, and noting the *context* (tired, stressed, caffeinated) rather than obsessing over the sensation itself. That actually helped somewhat. Seeing "left arm tingling — had been hunched over laptop for 3 hours" in writing made it harder to spiral into the worst-case story.

But I'm curious what others' experience has been. Did tracking help you or did it become compulsive? How do you keep it from becoming another anxiety ritual?
