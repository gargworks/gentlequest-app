I'm wondering what you actually recommend and why. Specifically:

- Do you prefer apps that are structured (CBT exercises, guided modules) or open-ended (journaling, notes)?

- Do you avoid recommending apps with streak/gamification features? I've found those counterproductive personally but I wonder if that's a clinical consideration too.
- Are there apps you've stopped recommending and why?

I currently use an app for between-session thought records and notes that's local-only, no streaks, no AI analysis. It works for me but I'm curious whether therapists generally have opinions on the privacy/streak/AI dimensions or if that's more of a user preference thing.

Not looking for a specific recommendation for myself — more curious about how you all think about this category.
