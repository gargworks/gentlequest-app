I've dealt with generalized anxiety for years and honestly never thought tracking it would help. Felt like one more thing to manage. But a few weeks ago I started just jotting down what was happening around me when my anxiety spiked — not a formal journal, just quick notes.

The thing that surprised me: my worst spikes weren't tied to the stuff I *thought* was stressing me out. They were clustered around mid-afternoon on weekdays, often after I'd skipped lunch. I was blaming work deadlines when half of it was probably just blood sugar and caffeine crashes.

I've been using a simple app to log this (free one, no streaks pushing me to perform) and the pattern became obvious within about 10 days. Not saying it's a cure — I still have bad days — but seeing the actual data instead of my anxious brain's narrative has been weirdly grounding.

Has anyone else done something like this? Did you find patterns that surprised you?
