I want to share something that's helped me during hard stretches, carefully and with the usual caveats. I'm not in crisis right now but I have been before, and during those times my brain is not reliable about remembering what helps.

A therapist helped me create a safety plan — a written document with: my warning signs, things I can do to cope, people I can reach out to, professionals and crisis lines, and reasons to stay. The key was having it accessible *before* I needed it, because in the moment I can't think clearly enough to assemble it.

I keep it on my phone in an app I can open in seconds. No login, no scrolling, no friction. When things get dark, I open it and it's all there — I don't have to remember anything, just follow what past-me wrote.

This isn't a magic fix. It's a life raft. But having the life raft assembled before the storm is the thing. If you don't have one and you have a therapist, I'd really encourage asking them to help you build one. If you don't have a therapist right now, you can still write one yourself.
