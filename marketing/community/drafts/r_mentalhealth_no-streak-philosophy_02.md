Every mental health app I've tried has this same feature: a streak counter. "You've meditated 7 days in a row!" "Don't break your streak!" And when you inevitably miss a day because you're, you know, dealing with mental health issues, it feels like failure.

This is backwards. The whole point of a mental health practice is that it's there for you when you need it. Making it about maintaining an unbroken chain turns it into another performance, another thing to feel guilty about skipping. I've talked to people who kept meditating out of streak anxiety rather than actual benefit. That's not wellness — that's a new anxiety on top of the old ones.

The app I use now has no streaks. No "you missed yesterday." No flame icon that dies if you take a break. I can use it daily for a month and then not touch it for two weeks and it doesn't care. It's just there when I want it.

I realize streaks work for some people and that's fine. But I think the default in mental health apps should be the opposite — low pressure, no guilt, use it when it helps. Am I alone in this?
