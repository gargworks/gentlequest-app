Unpopular opinion maybe: I can't stand journaling prompts. Every app and book is full of them. "What are you grateful for?" "Describe your ideal day." "If you could talk to your younger self..."

They're not bad. They're just not how my brain works. When I sit down to journal, I usually have something specific on my mind and a prompt about gratitude feels like being asked to do a different task than the one I showed up for. It makes journaling feel like school.

What works for me: a completely blank page. No prompt. No suggested topic. No "today's reflection is..." I just write whatever's in my head, even if it's "I don't know what to write" until something real comes out.

I found an app that lets me do this — no forced prompts, no daily question, just a blank entry. It's the first time journaling has felt like mine instead of an assignment.

Anyone else prefer the no-prompt approach? I know prompts help a lot of people get started but I wonder if some of us are forcing a method that doesn't fit.
