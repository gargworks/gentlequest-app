I've tried every sleep hygiene recommendation. Dark room, cool temperature, no screens, magnesium, tart cherry juice, weighted blanket, white noise. Some helped a little. None addressed the actual problem: my brain wouldn't shut up.

I'd lie in bed and my mind would run through tomorrow's tasks, replay today's awkward moments, generate random anxieties. The more I tried to not think, the more I thought. Classic.

What actually helped: spending 5-10 minutes writing down everything in my head before getting into bed. Not a journal entry — a brain dump. Every pending thing, every worry, every random thought. Just getting it out of my head and onto a page.

The effect: my brain seems to stop holding onto things once they're written down. Like it trusts the page to hold them instead of needing to actively remember them. I fall asleep noticeably faster on nights I do this vs. nights I don't.

It's not a cure — I still have bad nights. But it's been more effective than any supplement or gadget I've bought. Free, too, which is more than I can say for the $80 weighted blanket.
