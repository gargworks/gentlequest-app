I've had panic attacks on and off for about six years. For the longest time my instinct was to fight them — tensing up, trying to think my way out, googling symptoms (we all know how that goes).

What's actually helped me more recently is the opposite: just labeling what's happening in the moment. Not analyzing it, not reasoning with it, just naming it. "Heart racing. That's a sensation. It will pass." Sometimes out loud if I'm alone.

I started keeping a quick log afterward too — not a deep journal entry, just what was happening before, what the sensations were, how long it lasted. Over a few weeks I noticed my attacks rarely last more than 12-15 minutes even when they feel eternal. Knowing that number somehow makes the next one less terrifying.

This isn't medical advice — panic attacks can feel like something more serious and if you're unsure, get checked out. Not a diagnosis, see a professional. But for those of you who've been through the "is this a panic attack or am I dying" loop, has anything helped you sit with it rather than resist it?
