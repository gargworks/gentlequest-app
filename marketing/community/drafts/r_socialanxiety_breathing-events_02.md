I've had social anxiety my whole life and the worst part is the anticipatory period — the 30 minutes before a social thing where my heart is racing, I'm rehearsing conversations, and I'm seriously considering canceling.

I started doing a simple breathing routine before events and it's made a noticeable difference. Not a meditation session — just 2 minutes of slow breathing, usually box breathing (4 in, 4 hold, 4 out, 4 hold) or just longer exhales than inhales.

The key for me was doing it *deliberately as preparation*, not as a general practice. I sit in my car or in the bathroom or wherever I can be alone for 2 minutes before walking in. It doesn't eliminate the anxiety but it takes the edge off enough that I can function instead of being in full fight-or-flight from the first handshake.

I also write a quick note about what I'm nervous about beforehand — the specific fear, not just "everything." And then after, I note what actually happened. The gap between predicted disaster and actual reality is slowly becoming evidence that I can handle these things.
