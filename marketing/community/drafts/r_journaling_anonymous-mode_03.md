I noticed something interesting. When I journal in an app that's tied to my email or has my name on it, I edit myself. Not consciously — but there's a subtle awareness that this is *my* journal, attached to *my* identity, and if someone ever saw it, they'd know it was me.

I started using an app that requires no account — no email, no name, nothing. Just open and write. And my entries changed. They got more honest. More raw. I write about things I'd been avoiding even in my "private" journal because the layer of identity was still there.

It's like the difference between writing in a diary with your name on the cover vs. an unmarked notebook. The content shouldn't change but it does.

Has anyone else experienced this? I'm curious if it's just me or if the identity layer in most apps subtly shapes what people are willing to write.
