# Comet Trend Scout Protocol

**Role:** The Trend Scout (Browser Agent)
**Objective:** Use the Perplexity Web Interface (Zero Cost) to find high-signal trends.
**When to Run:** Daily (Morning).

---

## 🏁 Step 1: The "Search" (Browser Action)

**Agent Instruction:**
> "Go to `https://www.perplexity.ai` and ask the following prompt:"

### The Prompt
> "Scan the last 24 hours of discussions on r/ADHD, r/SaaS, and Twitter. What are the top 3 'Complaints' or 'Anxieties' developers or founders are talking about? Focus on burnout, focus, or AI fatigue. Summarize the 'Emotional Vibe' and suggest a counter-narrative."

---

## 🚀 Step 2: Handoff to Intelligence Portal (Seamless)

**Agent Instruction:**
1.  **Navigate** your browser to `http://localhost:9999/`.
2.  **Locate** the "Agent Drop-Zone" section.
3.  **Paste** the Perplexity summary into the "Raw Intelligence" box.
4.  **Select** "Trend Scout 📡" in the dropdown.
5.  **Click** the "Save to Brain" button.

---

## ✅ Step 3: Verify
1.  **Verify** the status message says "✅ Success: Saved".
2.  **Notify:** Tell the user "Trends scanned. Dashboard updated."

---

## ⚠️ Advantage
*   **Zero Cost:** Uses the free web interface.
*   **Live Web:** Access to real-time search index.
