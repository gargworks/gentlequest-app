About

We built GentleQuest because mental health software shouldn't feel like a job.

A quiet space to log how you're feeling and find resources that match where you are. Not therapy. Not medical care. A wellness companion for heavy moments.

How we build

Five principles, every screen.

Warmth over utility

Every string written for a person in a low moment. Clinical is never cold.

Skip anything, no shame

Every step has a skip. Every input is optional. Coming back is never penalized.

One clear next action

One primary CTA per screen. Choice is a kindness, not a maze.

Coral, not red

Even errors stay warm. Alarm colors belong to actual alarms, not to UI states.

Privacy is visible, not footnoted

Anonymity mode in the first viewport of Settings. "Stays on your device" said plainly, often.

What we don't do

No engagement loops that drain you.

×No streaks
×No levels
×No scores
×No diagnoses
×No data sales
