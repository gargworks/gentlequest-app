Product description

What GentleQuest is.

GentleQuest is a wellness companion for heavy moments. Not therapy. Not medical care. A space to log how you're feeling and find resources that match where you are — a calm chat that listens without prescribing, a journal that stays on your phone, a weekly review that shows patterns rather than scores, and a crisis lane that never blocks the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Built for adults in the United States as a first market; privacy-first by default.
